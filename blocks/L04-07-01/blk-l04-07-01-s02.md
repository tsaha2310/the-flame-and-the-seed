> **The Lens says:** Nucleotides drawn as three linked pieces at a hundred million times. The chain is drawn straight for now. The bench reads bases as letters because chemists do; the molecule has no letters on it.

Take an A nucleotide. Take a G. Bring the phosphate of the G to the sugar of the A, at the free hydroxyl on the sugar's third carbon. Join. Catch the water.

Add C. Add U. Add ten more in any order. Read the letters the bench prints.

Rotate the chain. Find the end with a free phosphate and the end with a free sugar hydroxyl.

Build a second chain of the same nucleotides in a different order. Compare.
