All three burned to almost the same gases: carbon dioxide and water, with a little nitrogen and sulphur gas from the seeds. All three left a pinch of ash: potassium, calcium, magnesium, a trace of iron. The oil left almost no ash at all.

The elements readout: carbon, hydrogen, oxygen in every sample, most of the mass. Nitrogen, phosphorus and sulphur in both seeds. A few metals in tiny amounts.

The live seed and the boiled seed gave the same list to two decimal places.
