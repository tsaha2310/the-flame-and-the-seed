**Predict first:** what does a deletion do now?

<!-- STEP id="run" -->
**Turn the dial.** With a marker between codons, a deleted letter leaves a two-letter codon that pairs with nothing; the ribosome skips it and reads the rest correctly. Frameshifts vanish. But the markers take up a quarter of the tape, and, worse, a mutation in a marker now scrambles a codon boundary. The sim's cell has traded one kind of damage for another and a longer tape.

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** Reading in threes with no markers is compact and makes frameshifts possible. Real cells chose compact, and use the start codon to set the frame.
