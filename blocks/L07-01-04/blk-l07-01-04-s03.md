**Predict first:** in each case, what does the blood insulin read, and does injecting insulin help?

<!-- STEP id="run" -->
**Turn the dial.** No insulin cells: blood glucose climbs after every meal and stays high; insulin in blood reads zero; the kidneys spill glucose into urine, water follows it by osmosis, and the body is thirsty all day. Inject insulin and the line falls to normal: the receptors were fine. Deaf receptors: blood glucose climbs the same way; but insulin in blood reads high, the pancreas is shouting and nobody hears; urine and thirst the same. Inject insulin and little changes: more shouting at deaf ears. Ten years of high sugar, either way: vessels, eyes, kidneys and nerves damaged.

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** The same symptom can come from a missing message or a missing receiver. Telling them apart is the whole of diagnosis, and the treatment differs completely.
