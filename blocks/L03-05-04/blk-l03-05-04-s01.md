Morning. The cucumber slice in salt is limp, swimming in its own water. The raisin in plain water is fat and smooth.

"The cucumber lost water," says Kabir. "The raisin gained it. But nobody poured any."

"The skins inside them chose," says Ajji. "Not the skin you can see. Smaller ones, far too small for eyes. You do not have the word yet. Call them sieves."
