Five mangoes. Kabir is not counting, because he has stopped thinking of it as winning.

"Every enzyme is a chain of beads in an exact order," he says. "Thousands of enzymes. And the seedling made every one of them from nothing but sugar, air and dirt. Something in there has the order of every bead of every protein written down."

"It does," says Ajji. "You built the molecule it is written on in Book IV. Two strands, the width of a rung, tough enough to last years in a dabba. Book VI is how it is read."

She takes the lens off its chain and hands it to him.

"The nucleus," she says. "Not the atom's. The other one."
