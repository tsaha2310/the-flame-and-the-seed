Kabir's world without parasites is a world of clones. He adds one parasite back and the clones fall.

**Predict first:** what happens to a sexual host population competing with a clonal one, with no parasites?

<!-- STEP id="run" -->
**Turn the dial.** Without parasites, the clones win. They reproduce twice as fast, because every clone is a mother and needs no father, and the sexual population is out-bred within a few hundred generations. Add parasites back, and the clones are caught by one key and collapse, while the sexual hosts persist.

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** Sex has a cost, half the population making no offspring directly, and a benefit that only shows when something is chasing you. Most living things are chased.
