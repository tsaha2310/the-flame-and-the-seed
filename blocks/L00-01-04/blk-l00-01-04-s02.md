> **The Lens says:** This is the first honesty card. Every time the Lens shows you something, it will tell you what it has changed. Here: sizes are true to scale on the ladder but not to each other in the pictures. Colour is real for the top rungs and made up below the width of a hair.

The ladder has a rung for every "ten times smaller".

- Top rung: the verandah, about 10 metres.
- Next: Kabir, about 1 metre.
- Next: your hand, about 10 centimetres.
- Next: the moong seed, about 5 millimetres.
- Next: the root hairs, thinner than a tenth of a millimetre.
- Below that: dark, for now.

Drag each object onto its rung. Then keep going down the ladder past where your eyes stop. The Lens will show you a rung only when a later Book has given you its words.
