**Predict first:** in each jar, what runs out first, food or oxygen?

**Turn the dial.** Dark jar: the plant burns its store; the snail eats the plant; oxygen falls; both die in weeks. No-carbon-dioxide jar: full light, but RuBisCO has nothing to attach; the plant burns its store in the light, making oxygen from nothing, until the store is gone; the snail's breath is the only carbon dioxide, and it is not enough. Earth: the food in every leaf, grain store and animal is eaten within a year or two, and everything that eats dies. The oxygen lasts far longer: the atmosphere holds a store of it so vast that breathing and burning would take thousands of years to use it. The order of failure is food first, air last.

**What this nails:** Photosynthesis is the only door through which carbon enters the living world and the only source of the oxygen that burns it. Everything else in this Book is downstream of a leaf.
