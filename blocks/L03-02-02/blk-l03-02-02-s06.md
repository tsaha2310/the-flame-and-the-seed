Kabir's small mole cannot be weighed. He tries a bigger one and lands, after some tries, near Avogadro's number, because that is what the balance needs.

**Predict first:** how much would a mole of hydrogen weigh in this world?

**Turn the dial.** A thousand hydrogen atoms weigh about a million-billion-billionth of a gram. No balance on Earth can see it. The recipe for water cannot be weighed out.

**What this nails:** The mole is huge on purpose. It is the smallest count that turns atoms into grams a balance can read.
