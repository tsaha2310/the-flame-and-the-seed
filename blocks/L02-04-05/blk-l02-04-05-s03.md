The oil: almost entirely shared pairs, carbon to carbon and carbon to hydrogen. Barely polar. Held to itself by dispersion forces only. No ions, no hydrogen bonds.

The live seed: shared pairs everywhere, most of them carbon to carbon, carbon to hydrogen, carbon to oxygen, carbon to nitrogen. Plenty of polar bonds. A great many hydrogen bonds, most of them in water, because a live seed is more than half water. A sprinkling of ions: potassium, magnesium, calcium, and some ions carrying phosphorus.

The boiled seed: the same census. The Lens found the same grips in the same proportions. A few hydrogen bonds sit in different places, but the totals match.
