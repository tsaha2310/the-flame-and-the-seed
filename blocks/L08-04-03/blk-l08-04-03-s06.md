Kabir's no-environment world makes identical twins exactly the same height. In the real one, they differ by a centimetre or two, which they do.

**Predict first:** what does the chart look like, and can two people with the same genes differ?

<!-- STEP id="run" -->
**Turn the dial.** With a hundred genes and no environment, the chart is still a smooth hill, since a hundred coins give many sums. But identical genotypes now give identical heights. In the real run, with environment on, the same genotype spreads over several centimetres depending on childhood food and illness. The sim marks the difference as the part of height that is not inherited.

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** Phenotype is genotype plus environment. For many-gene traits, the environment's share is large, and no square predicts an individual.
