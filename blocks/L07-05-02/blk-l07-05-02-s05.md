Kabir's plant without xylem is a moss. He puts the pipes back and it stands up.

**Predict first:** how tall can the plant grow?

<!-- STEP id="run" -->
**Turn the dial.** With no xylem, water reaches only the cells a few millimetres from the soil, by diffusion, from III.5. The leaf above dries and dies. The plant is a green smear at ground level. Mosses, which have no true xylem, are exactly this height.

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** Pipes are what let a plant be taller than diffusion allows. Xylem is why there are trees.
