Blood sugar that stays high because the insulin thermostat has failed is diabetes. Two main kinds.

In one, the body's own defences destroy the insulin cells, usually in childhood. No insulin is made. Blood sugar climbs, spills into urine with water following, from V.2 L04, and the body, unable to take glucose into its cells, burns fat instead and wastes. Insulin injections replace the missing message, and life goes on.

In the other, far more common, insulin is made but the receptors and their relays respond weakly. The pancreas makes more and more; the cells hear less and less. It comes on slowly in adults and is linked to weight, diet and inheritance, and India has more of it than almost anywhere. Exercise and diet make cells hear better; medicines help; injected insulin helps less than in the first kind, because the message is not what is missing.

Both kinds, uncontrolled, damage the body by the same route: years of sugar too high.

**Diabetes is the sugar thermostat broken. Remove the hormone and injecting it cures the symptom. Remove the receiver's hearing and the same symptom needs a different fix. Same sign, two diseases.**
