The powerhouse makes thirty coins per glucose, but it needs oxygen at the bottom of every staircase, and oxygen arrives through the blood only as fast as you breathe and pump. Its coins per second are capped by oxygen supply. Burning with oxygen is aerobic: "with air".

Fermentation makes two coins per glucose, but it needs no oxygen and its ten steps are fast. It can make coins as fast as glucose can be pulled from the sugar bank, from V.5. Its coins per second are far higher, for a short while. Burning without oxygen is anaerobic: "without air". The cost is glucose: fifteen times more per coin, and lactate piling up until the burn stops you.

A sprint is anaerobic: coins now, pay later. A marathon is aerobic: thirty per glucose, paced to the oxygen. The middle distances are a mix, and the art of running is choosing it.

**Aerobic: thirty coins per glucose, capped by oxygen. Anaerobic: two coins per glucose, fast, capped by lactate. Sprint on the short pipeline, marathon on the long one.**

Every cell in the seedling runs the long one, because a plant never sprints. Except its roots in waterlogged soil, which ferment, and can drown in their own alcohol.
