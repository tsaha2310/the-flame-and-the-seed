Book II opens with Ajji handing Kabir a hammer, which is not a sentence anyone expected.

On the chopping board: a chunk of rock salt from the pickle jar, and a small square of copper sheet from the electrician's box.

"Hit them," she says.

Kabir hits the copper. It dents. He hits it again. It bends and dents more, and stays in one piece.

He hits the rock salt. It explodes into a hundred tiny cubes.

"Same hammer," says Ajji. "Same boy. Why?"
