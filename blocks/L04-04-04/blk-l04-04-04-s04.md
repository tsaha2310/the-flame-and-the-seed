A bond ledger from III.4 cannot tell rice from cellulose. Same bonds, same count, same energy on burning. The balance cannot tell them apart either; same mass. The elements are identical. The grips are identical.

The gut can tell them apart, instantly. The cutter that snips alpha links slides off beta links. It is shaped for one and not the other, and the shape of a link is a difference of a few degrees in where one oxygen points.

So the moong seed stores starch, alpha, and can get at it when it sprouts. It builds its walls from cellulose, beta, and nothing inside the seed can eat the walls by mistake. Same bead, two jobs, sorted by shape.

**Energy is not food. Food is energy in a shape your cutters fit. From here on, the shape of a molecule is the whole story.**

That sentence is the doorway to the next rung: nitrogen, and the molecules that do the cutting.
