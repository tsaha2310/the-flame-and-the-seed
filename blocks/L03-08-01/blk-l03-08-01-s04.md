Four coins: sixteen ways. Only one gives four heads. Six give two and two. Two-and-two is six times more likely, not because any toss prefers it, but because there are more ways to get it.

Ten coins: one way to get ten heads. Two hundred and fifty-two ways to get five and five.

A hundred coins: one way to get all heads. About a hundred billion billion billion ways to get fifty-fifty. Toss a hundred coins a billion times a second for the age of the universe and you will never see all heads.

The ink is the same. One arrangement has all the ink on the left of the jug. A number too large to write has it spread evenly. Each molecule is walking randomly, and randomly means every arrangement is as likely as every other. But there are so many more spread-out arrangements than gathered ones that spread-out is what you find. Always.

The number of ways a state can be arranged has a name: entropy. More ways, higher entropy. A spread-out jug has enormously higher entropy than a gathered one.

**Entropy counts the number of ways. More ways means more likely. The ink spreads because spread has more ways than gathered.**

Entropy is not disorder or mess. A messy room is not high entropy in this sense; it is just a room. Entropy is a count, and the count is what tips the world.
