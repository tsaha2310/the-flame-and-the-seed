"Your hand came back from the diya," says Ajji, "in about a twentieth of a second. The message went from your fingertip to your spinal cord and back to your arm muscle, over a metre, in that time. Adrenaline in the blood takes twenty seconds. What can go that fast?"

"Electricity."

"A kind of it. Not electrons in a wire. Ions across a wall. Build the cell that carries it, and first make it quiet."
