The peanut. Burned under a cup, it gave 24 kilojoules of heat and nothing else. Eaten, the same 24 kilojoules, and Kabir ran between wickets on it.

"Same equation," says Ajji. "Same fall. The diya spends it in one drop and gets heat. Your cells spend it and get coins. What is different?"

"The cell has a wheel," says Kabir, from the waterwheel lesson.

"One wheel? Try it."
