The stream falling is exergonic. Free energy drops. Left alone, it would drop into heat and noise at the bottom of the fall.

The bucket rising is endergonic. Free energy rises. Left alone, it does not happen.

Connect them through the wheel, and the fall pays for the lift. The two changes have become one change, and that one change is downhill overall: the fall is bigger than the lift, and the difference is wasted as heat. Joining an exergonic change to an endergonic one so that the pair runs together is coupling.

Three rules the sim enforces, because nature does.

- The lift can never be bigger than the fall. Book I.
- Some of the fall is always wasted. Nothing couples perfectly.
- The two must be connected. A stream next to a bucket does nothing. The wheel is the connection.

**Coupling joins a fall to a lift through a shared connection. The pair runs downhill together, and the lift gets done.**

The seed makes sugar by coupling the making, uphill, to something falling. The seed builds itself by coupling every uphill step to a fall. What falls, and what the wheel is made of, is Book IV and Book V.
