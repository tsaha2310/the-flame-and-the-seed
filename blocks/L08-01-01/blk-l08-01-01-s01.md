"Watson and Crick ended their 1953 paper," says Ajji, "with one sentence: it has not escaped our notice that the pairing suggests a copying mechanism. Each strand is a template for the other, from Book IV. Unzip, build a partner on each, and you have two helices."

"So copying is obvious."

"The idea was obvious. Whether it was true was not, and three different ways were proposed. In 1958 Meselson and Stahl told them apart with the heavy atoms from Book I."
