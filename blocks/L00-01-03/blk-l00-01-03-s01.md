Day two. Kabir gets to the bowls first again.

"One of them is cheating," he says.

Two of the live seeds have cracked their coats. A tiny white point pokes out of each one. The boiled seeds are fatter and softer than yesterday, and that is all.

"They were the same," says Kabir. "Same seeds. Same water. Same bowl. I boiled them for five minutes. Five minutes! And now these are dead?"

He picks up a boiled seed and squeezes it. It squashes like a cooked pea.
