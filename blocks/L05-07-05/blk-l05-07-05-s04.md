Three lessons from three failures.

Block the last puller: electrons cannot fall, so nothing upstream runs. Oxygen is useless if nothing can hand it electrons. Cyanide kills this way in minutes.

Holes in the dam: the fall still happens, but it becomes heat instead of coins. Oxygen is used faster than ever. Newborn babies, and hibernating animals, have a special fat with a leak protein built into its mitochondrial dams on purpose: brown fat. It burns sugar and fat to make heat directly, no shivering needed, which is why a baby wrapped in a blanket stays warm without moving. A leak is a short circuit used as a heater.

Jam the turbine: the dam fills and backs everything up. Nothing burns, nothing is made.

**Energy from food goes into a gradient, and the gradient makes ATP. Break the gradient's use and the fall becomes heat. Brown fat does this on purpose to keep a baby warm.**
