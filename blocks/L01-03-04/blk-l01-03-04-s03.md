> **The Lens says:** The ladder is the Hill for an electron, drawn as rungs. Electrons do not sit on rungs or circle the nucleus. Light is drawn as a small wavy arrow; real light has no arrow. Time is slowed by a million million.

Pick sodium. Heat it gently. Watch an electron jump to a higher rung and fall back. Read the colour strip.

Heat it hard. Do the colours change, or only how many packets come out?

Try hydrogen. Try potassium. Then try to make sodium give a colour it has not already given.
