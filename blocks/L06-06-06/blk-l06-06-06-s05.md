Six mangoes. The count has become a joke between them.

"A thermostat," says Kabir. "It senses heat and responds. The seed's cells sense things and flip switches. What is the difference?"

"That," says Ajji, "is Book VII. It starts with you touching a hot diya, and your hand pulling back before you feel it. And it ends with a squid that glows, ants that vote, and a forest that may or may not be talking underground."

She lights the diya, the same brass one from the first night. Kabir keeps his hands in his pockets.
