Kabir's equal-atom world has 3 g moles of water. His mouthful is a sip.

**Predict first:** in this world, does 18 g of water still contain one mole?

<!-- STEP id="run" -->
**Turn the dial.** With every atom at mass 1, water weighs 3 per molecule and the seed's sugar 24. One mole of water is 3 g. The whole molar mass table is different, and the recipe for burning methane is different in grams.

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** Molar mass depends on the real, unequal masses of atoms. The shopkeeper's trick needs the coins to be what they are.
