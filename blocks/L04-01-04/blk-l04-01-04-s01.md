Ajji draws a zigzag on the board. Four bends. No letters.

"That is a molecule," she says. "Tell me what it is."

"It is a zigzag."

"Chemists are lazy. They stopped writing the carbons and hydrogens a hundred years ago. Every corner is a carbon. Every end is a carbon. The hydrogens are wherever they must be to give each carbon four bonds. So: how many carbons? How many hydrogens?"
