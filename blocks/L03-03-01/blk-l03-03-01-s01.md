Kabir cut an apple at breakfast and forgot half of it. By lunch the cut face is brown.

The garden gate has been rusting for as long as anyone remembers. Ajji runs a finger along it and it comes away orange.

"Same thing," she says.

"An apple is not iron," says Kabir.

"No. But something the same happened to both. And it is what happens to the steel wool, and to the diya, and, very slowly, to you."
