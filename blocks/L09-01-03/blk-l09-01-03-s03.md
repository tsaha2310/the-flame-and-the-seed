The eight cards sort cleanly now. The flame: energy falling, and nothing else. The salt crystal: a pattern repeating, no tape, no fire. Dahi: full of bacteria, each a closed loop. The cloud: a pattern of water and air. The robot vacuum: a program and a battery, no copying, no kind. The live seed: every arrow. The boiled seed: the tape, and machines unfolded; every other arrow empty.

Four cards the loop does not settle.

The virus: a tape in a coat. It copies, and its kind changes fast, from VIII.5. It has no wall of its own, no slow fire, no machines; it borrows all three from a cell. The loop, with the machines on loan.

The mule: every arrow but one. Its cells are full loops. Its line does not continue. Alive as an animal, closed at the generation.

The dry seed in the dabba: every arrow present and every arrow paused. No energy caught, nothing built, for years. Then water, and all of it starts. The loop, stopped and restartable, which the boiled seed is not.

The unbuilt machine: a wall of metal, energy from a socket, a program it copies with errors, machines it builds from the program, including its own builders. If it were built, every arrow would be filled, and it would be made of no carbon at all.

The seven signs were what a closed loop does when it runs: it takes in fuel, uses oxygen, builds, moves, responds, copies, and eventually stops. They are true and they are downstream. Ajji's page was right about the symptoms and silent about the cause.

**The loop settles the flame, the crystal, the cloud and the vacuum. It leaves four cards open: a virus, which borrows its loop; a mule, whose loop does not continue; a dry seed, whose loop is paused; and a machine that no one has built.**
