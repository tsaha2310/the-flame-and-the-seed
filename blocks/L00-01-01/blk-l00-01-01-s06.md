Ajji goes inside and comes back with three things.

A small brass lens on a chain, heavier than it looks. Her old notebook, the cover soft with age. And one moong seed, which she puts in your palm.

"Ajji was a scientist," Kabir whispers, as if you did not know.

She opens the notebook. Three columns ruled in her own hand: Flame. Live seed. Boiled seed. The last page has been torn out. You can see the ragged edge.

"The rains last three months," she says. "So does this seed. Find me a difference that Kabir cannot break."

Kabir grins. He likes breaking things.

Tonight: put ten moong seeds to soak in a bowl. Put ten more in a second bowl, but boil those first, for five minutes, and let them cool. Label the bowls. Do not swap them.
