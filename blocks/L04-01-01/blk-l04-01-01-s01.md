The VITAL FORCE card has been on the wall since the Prologue. Kabir has drawn a question mark on it, and then a second one.

"Until 1828," says Ajji, "everyone knew that the stuff of living things could only be made by living things. Sugar came from cane. Oil came from seeds. Urea, the stuff in urine, came from kidneys. You could not make any of it in a flask, and the reason was the vital force. Only life had it."

"Then what happened in 1828?"

"A German chemist called Friedrich Wohler was trying to make something else entirely."
