Kabir's one-to-one relay releases a few beads per messenger. He restores the amplification and the flood returns.

**Predict first:** how much glucose leaves per messenger?

<!-- STEP id="run" -->
**Turn the dial.** One messenger activates one receptor, one enzyme, one of the next, and one phosphorylase. A few glucose beads leave. To get a million, the blood would have to deliver a million messengers to that one cell, and the gland that makes them would have to be the size of the liver.

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** Amplification is how a whisper from a gland moves a whole body. Without it, hormones would have to be shouted, by the kilogram.
