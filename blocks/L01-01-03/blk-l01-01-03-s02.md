> **The Lens says:** The sieve, the hot plate and the wires are drawn at real size. What comes off the sample under the wires is drawn as bubbles a thousand times bigger than life.

Each sample is a black box. You have three tools.

- The sieve and magnet pull apart things that are only sitting together.
- Evaporation boils water away and shows what was hiding in it.
- Electric current is the hardest tool. Passed through some things, it splits them into new things.

Attack every sample with every tool. Write down which tools work on which samples, and what comes out.
