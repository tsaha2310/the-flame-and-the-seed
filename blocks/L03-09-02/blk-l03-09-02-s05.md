Kabir's copper-copper battery reads zero. He is not surprised, and says so. Ajji tells him that being unsurprised by the right thing is most of science.

**Predict first:** what does the meter read?

**Turn the dial.** Zero, for every metal. Two rungs at the same height have no gap. No push, no current, no LED. The sim then shows zinc against zinc in two different liquids, and a small voltage appears: the liquids made a difference where the metals did not.

**What this nails:** A battery is a difference. No difference, no volts. Later, a living membrane makes volts from a difference in ions alone, with no metal at all.
