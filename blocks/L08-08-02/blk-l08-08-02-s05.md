Kabir's lossless web feeds a mountain-sized predator that, by its own rules, cannot be alive.

**Predict first:** how long can a chain be?

<!-- STEP id="run" -->
**Turn the dial.** With no loss, chains run to any length, and the sim builds a twenty-step chain with a top predator the size of a hill. Then it flags the violation: an eater that keeps everything has not burned anything, and an animal that burns nothing is a boiled seed, from V.6. The rule was the Hill's, not the garden's.

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** The tenfold loss is the cost of being alive at each step. It cannot be engineered away, because it is the same waste that keeps every cell above the valley floor.
