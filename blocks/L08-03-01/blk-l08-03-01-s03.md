> **The Lens says:** A cell with two pairs of chromosomes, drawn large; a real moong has eleven pairs and a human twenty-three. Pollen and egg are drawn as the same kind of cell for simplicity. Generations pass in seconds.

Make sex cells by mitosis. Count them. Fertilise. Count the child. Run three generations.

Switch to the halving dance. Watch it. Count the sex cells. Fertilise. Count the child. Run three generations.

Look at which chromosomes each sex cell gets: one of each pair, or two of one and none of the other?
