The potato contains something that gives the peroxide a different, lower path to the same products. The hump is lower, so at room temperature many more collisions cross. The potato is not changed and not used up. It is a catalyst.

A catalyst lowers the hump. It does not lower the valley. The products sit exactly as low as they did before, and the energy out per crossing is exactly the same. Only the rate changes.

And a catalyst lowers the hump from both sides. It speeds the reverse reaction just as much. It cannot make a reaction go somewhere it would not have gone anyway; it only gets it there faster.

**A catalyst lowers the hump and never the hill. It changes how fast, never how far.**

That sentence is the guard rail for all of Book V. The seed burns sugar at room temperature because it is full of catalysts, thousands of different ones, each lowering one hump. None of them changes where the valley is.
