Ajji looks at the bench's smallest passing set for a long time.

"That," she says, "is about what the first cell had. A wall, a slow fire, a tape, and machines made of the tape that copy the tape. Nothing else. Everything else, the senses, the bodies, the forest, came after."

"And the flame?"

"Tomorrow you take the loop apart one part at a time and see what each failure looks like. One of the failures is the flame. One is the boiled seed. And one is the diya's oil sitting in the tin."
