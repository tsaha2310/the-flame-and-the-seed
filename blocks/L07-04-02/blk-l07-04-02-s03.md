Seal the air and B does nothing, though soil and roots are shared. Share the air and separate the pots, and B responds. The message travels through the air. A chewed maize leaf releases small, light hydrocarbons, from IV.2, that evaporate at once and drift. The wasp reads the same plume, from downwind.
