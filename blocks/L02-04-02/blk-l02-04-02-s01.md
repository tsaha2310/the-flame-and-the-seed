Five cards on the table, no numbers on them.

- Methane: one carbon, four hydrogens, a tetrahedron. Barely polar.
- Water: bent, two hydrogen bonds per molecule at least.
- Salt: a checkerboard lattice of ions.
- Iron: a lattice of ions in a sea of electrons.
- Diamond: every carbon shared-pair bonded to four others, in all directions, with no end.

"Rank them," says Ajji. "Lowest boiling point to highest. Then I show you the numbers."
