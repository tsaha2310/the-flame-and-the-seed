Taking apart runs downhill. Starch to glucose, fat to fatty acids, glucose to carbon dioxide and water: exergonic, from III.8. These are catabolic reactions. Cata means down. Their fall is where the coins come from.

Building runs uphill. Amino acids to a protein chain, nucleotides to RNA, carbon dioxide and water to sugar: endergonic. These are anabolic reactions. Ana means up. Each one must be coupled to a fall, and the fall is nearly always an ATP breaking, from IV.6.

The whole set, thousands of reactions, catabolic and anabolic, running together in one cell, with the falls of the first paying for the climbs of the second, is metabolism. The word means change. It is what the slow fire is.

**Catabolic reactions run downhill and take things apart. Anabolic reactions run uphill and build. Metabolism is all of them together, with the falls paying for the climbs through ATP.**

The flame is all catabolic. It takes the oil apart and builds nothing. The seed does both, and that is the shape of the rest of Book V.
