The oil: fats. Triglycerides with long tails and nothing else. No sugars, no proteins, no tape.

The live seed: fats stored for the sprout. Starch in the store and cellulose in the walls. Thousands of kinds of protein, all folded, each with its pocket. RNA in short strands. DNA in long strands, intact.

The boiled seed: the same fats. The same starch and cellulose. The same thousands of kinds of protein, by sequence, and every one unfolded and tangled. RNA in pieces, but that happens in an hour in any seed. DNA, intact.

Ajji answers that one herself, for once. "Because a shape is not a thing. It is a position on the Hill, above the valley floor. The boiled seed's proteins slid down. Nothing in the seed can lift ten thousand tangled chains back up one by one. And the live seed's proteins are only folded because something keeps them there, and keeps making new ones, all the time, which is Book V."
