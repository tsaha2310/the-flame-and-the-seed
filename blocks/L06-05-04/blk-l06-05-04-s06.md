Ajji writes under Live seed: "the tape is behind a double wall with choosy pores; copies are cut and joined exactly, capped, tailed, and only then sent out; one gene can give several proteins."

"Every cell in the seedling has the same tape," says Kabir. "Root cells, leaf cells, the cells in the flower. But they are different. A root cell does not make chlorophyll."

"Same book," says Ajji. "Different pages open. That is the last module of this Book, and it starts with a frog grown from a gut cell."
