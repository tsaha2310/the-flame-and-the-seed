Kabir turns off sharing and the sky falls. He turns it back on quickly.

**Predict first:** what happens to water, sugar and the air in this world?

<!-- STEP id="run" -->
**Turn the dial.** Hydrogen and oxygen cannot form a shared pair, so water becomes a lattice of ions that is solid at room temperature. Sugar and oil do not exist. Nitrogen and oxygen in the air become ionic solids and fall out of the sky. The sim's kitchen is a pile of crystals.

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** Sharing is the grip that makes molecules. No sharing, no liquids at room temperature, no oil, no seed.
