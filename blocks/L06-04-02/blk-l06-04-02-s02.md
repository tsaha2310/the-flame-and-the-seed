> **The Lens says:** The two subunits drawn at a million times, cut open to show the slots; a real one is a dense knot of RNA and protein. The mRNA is a strip. Charged tRNAs arrive by diffusion; you place them. The joining step is real and is slowed a thousand times.

Load an mRNA. The small subunit finds AUG, from VI.3, and the large one clamps on. A tRNA carrying methionine sits in the middle slot.

Read the next codon in the right-hand slot. Find a charged tRNA whose anticodon pairs with it. Place it. Watch the check. Watch the bead on the middle tRNA get handed to the bead on the right-hand tRNA.

Shift: everything moves one slot left. The empty tRNA leaves from the left-hand slot. A new codon shows on the right.

Keep going to a stop codon. Then run a hundred codons with the supply of one tRNA turned low.
