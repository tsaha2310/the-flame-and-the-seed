Kabir's two-oily soap makes the plate greasier. His two-watery soap makes clean water and a greasy plate. He asks for the real one back.

**Predict first:** does it clean the grease?

<!-- STEP id="run" -->
**Turn the dial.** The two-oily molecules are squeezed out of the water, exactly like oil in II.3, and sit on top as a scum. They join the grease happily and then just float with it. Nothing is lifted; the grease is now bigger. The two-watery version dissolves in the water and ignores the grease completely. Only the real soap, one end each, lifts the grease off and carries it away.

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** Cleaning needs a molecule that belongs to both worlds. One end in the oil, one end in the water, and the water can carry the oil.
