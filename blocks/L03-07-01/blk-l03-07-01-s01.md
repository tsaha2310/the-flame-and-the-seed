A turmeric stain on Kabir's shirt from lunch. Ajji dabs it with soap. The yellow stain turns red.

"I made it worse!"

"You made it tell you something. Rinse it." Under the tap the red goes back to yellow.

Kabir remembers this from school. Turmeric is an indicator. Soap is a base. Nimbu is an acid. He can say the words.

"Good," says Ajji. "Now say what an acid is."

He cannot. Nobody in Class 7 could.
