> **The Lens says:** The track is drawn from the side, flat, like a hill in a drawing. The marble's speed is shown as a number no real marble wears. Air and rubbing are switched off unless you switch them on.

Build a track. Start high, go down, put a hill in the middle, end somewhere.

Before each run, predict where the marble will stop. Then release it.

Try to build a track where the marble climbs higher than it started. Keep trying until you are sure.
