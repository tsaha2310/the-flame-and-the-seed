Burn sodium in oxygen. Watch the cut-away. Watch where electrons go.

Burn sodium in chlorine. Watch the cut-away. Watch where electrons go.

Now burn in oxygen and, halfway, remove the oxygen and pipe in chlorine. Does the burning stop?
