Grease on a plate. Water alone runs off it. A drop of soap solution, and the grease lifts and swirls away.

"Soap is a fatty acid whose carboxyl has given its hydrogen ion to a base," says Ajji. "The chuna in a soap-maker's pot. So the end is now a charged carboxyl, and it loves water. The tail is still hydrocarbon, and it loves oil."

"Two loves," says Kabir.

"Watch what a molecule with two loves does when it meets grease."
