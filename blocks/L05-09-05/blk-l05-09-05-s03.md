Kabir's green-lit plant is pale and thin and alive. "Not dead. Just hungry."

"Now the real question," says Ajji. "Why is it built this way?"

**Predict first:** does the green-lit plant make any sugar at all?

<!-- STEP id="run" -->
**Turn the dial.** It grows a little. Not nothing. Chlorophyll absorbs a small share of green, and the leaf has helper pigments, orange and yellow ones, that catch some green and hand its energy to chlorophyll. The red-lit and blue-lit plants grow far better. The white-lit best of all. The dark one dies. Green is the worst light for a plant that is not darkness.

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** A leaf throws back most green light, not all. The pigments' rungs set what is caught, and the leaf is green because that colour is caught least.
