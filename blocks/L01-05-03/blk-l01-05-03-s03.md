> **The Lens says:** The shared pair of electrons is drawn as a rope with a knot. Electrons are not ropes. The atoms are drawn as two faces with arms, which is the story voice; the explain slide will say what really pulls. Pull strength is drawn as arm thickness.

Pick sodium against chlorine. Watch the knot.

Pick two chlorines. Watch the knot.

Pick carbon against hydrogen. Then carbon against oxygen. Then oxygen against hydrogen.

Now use the table: run across a row, left to right, keeping the other atom fixed. Then run down a column. Write down what changes the pull.
