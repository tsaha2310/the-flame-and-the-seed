> **The Lens says:** The bubbles are drawn ten times larger. The gases are given colours so you can tell them apart; both are invisible in life. The test tubes fill in seconds instead of minutes.

Put the upturned tubes over the leads and collect the gases until one tube is full.

Measure both tubes. What is the ratio?

Take the fuller tube to a flame: the pop test. Take the other tube to a glowing splint. Note what each does.

Do it three times. Get the ratio right each time to win.
