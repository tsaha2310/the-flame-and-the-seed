> **The Lens says:** Species as cards with real diets; arrows point from the eaten to the eater. Energy is drawn as a flow along each arrow, with the fraction that passes on set by the real figure of about a tenth. Seasons pass in seconds.

Place the plants. Run a season. Read the energy they capture from light.

Add the plant-eaters, with arrows. Run. Read the energy that reaches them.

Add the eaters of plant-eaters. Then their eaters. Run. Read the energy at each step.

Try to build a chain of seven steps. Watch what happens at the top.
