Kabir lets the pairs sort by count alone and most of his seeds do not sprout.

**Predict first:** how many children are viable?

<!-- STEP id="run" -->
**Turn the dial.** Gametes carry twenty-two, but some pairs are doubled and some missing. Most children are missing a chromosome or carrying three, and most of those fail to develop. A few by chance get one of each and live. The population shrinks generation by generation.

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** The pairs must separate as pairs. That precision is what meiosis's line-up step buys, and when it slips in one pair, the result is a real condition, in the last lesson of this module.
