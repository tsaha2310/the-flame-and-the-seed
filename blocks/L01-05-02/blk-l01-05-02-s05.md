Kabir's one-shell world has no families. Lithium and sodium have nothing in common. The chart on the wall would be a list.

**Predict first:** with one shell for everything, does the table still have columns?

<!-- STEP id="run" -->
**Turn the dial.** Every element now has all its electrons on the outside. Lithium has three outer electrons, sodium eleven, potassium nineteen. Nothing repeats. The table collapses into one long row with no families.
