Kabir turns the dial back to 104. The oceans refill. The salt dissolves. The coin holds thirty-one drops.

Ajji writes in the notebook, under all three columns: "held together by shared pairs; the water in it is bent."

"That is not a difference either," says Kabir.

"No. But you now know why the seed is wet inside, and why the oil in the diya is not. Tomorrow: why one thing is solid and another is liquid and another is gas, with the same grips."

She spreads ghee on a hot roti. It melts on the way to the plate.
