The wall's traffic is a loop. In by vesicle, to the lysosome; out by vesicle, from the workshop floor. Cut the out-leg and the loop stops turning. The wall shrinks because it is still being swallowed. The products pile up because their only exit was the fusing of a vesicle with the outer wall. And the cell falls silent, because everything it says to other cells, it says by spitting.

The toxin that does this in real life cuts the proteins that let a vesicle fuse with the wall. In a nerve ending, that means the message to the muscle never leaves. Tiny amounts are used, carefully, to relax muscles that clench; larger amounts stop the muscles that breathe.

**Exocytosis is the cell's only way to send anything out. Block it, and the cell shrinks, fills up, and falls silent. A nerve's message is a bubble spat out.**
