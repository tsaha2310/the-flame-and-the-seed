Here is what the hinge nails down, in order.

Shape makes polarity. Two polar bonds only give a polar molecule if the shape stops them cancelling. Bent water is polar. Straight water is not.

Polarity makes the pulls between molecules. Polar water molecules hold each other with hydrogen bonds. Straight water would have only dispersion forces, like oil, and would boil far below zero.

Those pulls make the ocean. A boiling point of 100 degrees, instead of minus 80, is the entire difference between a planet of seas and a planet of steam.

And the same charged corners are what pull a salt crystal apart. That is Book III. And what the seed uses to keep its insides in. That is Book V.

**One bend of 76 degrees, in one small molecule, is the reason there is liquid water on Earth.**
