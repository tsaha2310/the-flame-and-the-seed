Two tools on the page now: the Hill, and charge. Put together, they give a rule: opposite charges near each other are low, and low is where things end up.

"Now," says Ajji, "if the flame's yellow light is a fall, what is falling?"

She sprinkles a pinch of salt from the shelf into the diya's flame. It flares bright yellow. Kabir yelps.

The salt did not burn. The yellow came from something inside it, and it came in a colour it could not have chosen. Tomorrow the lens goes into a grain of salt.
