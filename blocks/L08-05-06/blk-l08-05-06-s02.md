> **The Lens says:** A warm pool of nucleotides, drawn at a hundred million times; the molecules you build are RNA strands from IV.7. Copying by pairing is real chemistry, though slow; the folded copier is a hypothesis, drawn as a hairpin with a pocket. Generations pass in seconds.

Build a short strand. Let nucleotides pair against it. Watch a partner form, slowly. Let the partner template a copy of the original.

Fold a longer strand into a hairpin with a pocket, from IV.7 L03. Test whether the fold can join nucleotides faster than they join alone. Find a fold that copies strands, including itself.

Allow copying errors. Run a population of copiers for a thousand rounds. Watch which folds come to dominate.
