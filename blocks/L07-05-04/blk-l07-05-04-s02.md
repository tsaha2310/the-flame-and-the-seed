> **The Lens says:** The plant at true scale with its tip and buds; the signal from the tip drawn as a fading colour down the stem, a real hormone. The animal panel is a cartoon of skin with a wound and scattered cells that can divide.

Run the plant unpruned for a week. Watch the tip grow. Watch the side buds.

Prune the tip. Run a week. Watch the buds. Watch the shape.

Prune again, on each new branch. Shape the plant into a bush.

Switch to the animal. Make a wound. Run. Watch which cells divide, and where they came from.
