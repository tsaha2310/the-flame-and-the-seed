The power is off again, so the balloon from Kabir's birthday gets a new job.

Rub it on his hair. Hold it near the wall. It sticks. Hold it near a thin stream of water from the tap. The water bends toward it.

Rub a plastic comb and hold it over torn bits of paper. They jump up to it.

"The balloon is a magnet," says Kabir.

Ajji hands him a fridge magnet and a bit of paper. The magnet does nothing to the paper. "Not a magnet," she says. "Something else. Find its rules."
