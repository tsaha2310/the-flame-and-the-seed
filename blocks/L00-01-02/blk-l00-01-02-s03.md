> **The Lens says:** Days pass in seconds. Broth is drawn clear when fresh and cloudy when spoiled. The things that spoil it are drawn as dots; the real ones are a thousand times smaller than the dots.

Ajji lays out the replay. "Two men. Two hundred years apart. Same argument."

In 1668 Francesco Redi did the jar experiment. In 1859 Louis Pasteur did a harder one. He boiled meat broth in a glass flask and then bent the neck into an S shape. Air could get in. Dust could not: it settled in the bend.

Run all three flasks for thirty days. Watch which broths cloud over.

Then do what Pasteur did next. Snap the swan neck off one flask. Tilt another so the broth touches the dust in the bend. Run thirty more days.
