"Everything in the cell is protein. A protein-cutter has to be asleep until it leaves."

"And everything in the cell that cuts, joins, pumps or builds," says Ajji, "has a switch of some kind. The seedling is thousands of enzymes, each throttled by something. Nobody in charge."

**Predict first:** what happens to the stomach cell?
