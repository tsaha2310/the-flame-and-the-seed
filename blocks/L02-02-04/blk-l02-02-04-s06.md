The grip is done and the shapes are named. Steal, share, pool. Line, triangle, tetrahedron. Lone pairs that bend things. Carbon that goes on forever.

Kabir goes back to Book I's question, the one he wrote in the notebook. "So water is share, and so is the diya's oil. Why does oil sit on top of the dal and never mix in?"

Ajji spoons some ghee into a bowl of hot dal. It beads up into golden drops and refuses to dissolve.

"Because a share is not always fair," she says. "Tomorrow."
