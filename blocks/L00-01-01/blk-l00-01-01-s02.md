> **The Lens says:** Nothing is slowed or enlarged here. The cards are drawings. The mule and the virus are drawn to the same size, which is a lie of about a hundred million times.

Ajji has been listening from her chair. She puts eight cards on the table.

- a candle flame
- a moong seed from the dabba
- a salt crystal growing in a saucer of brine
- a mule
- a bowl of dahi
- a virus
- a robot vacuum cleaner
- a monsoon cloud

"Sort them," she says. "Alive on the left. Not alive on the right. Then write your rule in the box. One sentence. The rule must put every card where you put it."

Try it. Write a rule. Watch the Lens test your rule against all eight cards.
