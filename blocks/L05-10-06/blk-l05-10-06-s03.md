The engine: fuel in, about a tenth caught as work, the rest heat. The cell: fuel in, about a third caught as coins, the rest heat. Both have a load. Both catch part of a fall.

What the work is spent on. The engine: turning a wheel that does nothing to the engine. The cell: building more cell. Pumps to keep its inside different, enzymes to replace the ones that wear, wall to grow, and, in the leaf, the fuel itself, from air and light. The cell spends its caught energy on staying out of the valley and on making more of itself. The engine spends it on a flywheel.

A week later. The engine is as it was, worn a little, empty of fuel, waiting to be filled by someone. The cell has made copies of itself, replaced most of its parts, and, if it is green, made its own fuel from the air.
