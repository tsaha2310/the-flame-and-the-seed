Kabir's stiff RNA reads fine and does nothing. He has seen this trade before, with the flat carbon world.

**Predict first:** can RNA still do anything?

<!-- STEP id="run" -->
**Turn the dial.** The strand cannot bend back on itself, so no pairs form within it. It can still be read as a message. But it cannot fold into a clover leaf or a pocket. Every RNA tool in the sim's seed is a straight stick that does nothing.

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** The Tape is a message because of its letters and a machine because it can fold. Take away folding and half of what RNA does is gone, including, in Book VI, the machine that reads the message.
