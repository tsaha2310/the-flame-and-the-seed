Ajji cracks an egg into a hot pan. The clear part turns white and solid in seconds.

"Egg white is protein dissolved in water," she says. "Folded. Heat it and it is solid and cloudy and will never go clear again. The nimbu did the same to the milk."

Kabir looks at the pan, then at the jar of boiled-seed memory in his head. "The boiled seed."

"Yes. Now you have the words for it."
