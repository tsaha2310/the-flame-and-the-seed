Sunlight peaks in the green. A black leaf would catch all of it. Leaves are green, which means they throw back the very colour the sun delivers most of. On the face of it, a poor design.

Nobody built it, so there is no designer to ask. There are only reasons the green design could have won out, and scientists do not fully agree which matter most.

**Leaves are green because chlorophyll's rungs catch red and blue and miss green. Why that pigment and not a black one is a question with several honest answers, and it is still argued.**
