**Predict first:** when the food moves, do the ants find the new source?

<!-- STEP id="run" -->
**Turn the dial.** The old trail stays at full strength. Ants follow it to bare floor, turn round, and follow it back. A few wander and find the new food, but their fresh trail is no stronger than the permanent old one, so the colony never shifts. Most ants walk a loop to nothing until they starve. The plant: the alarm plume stays, B keeps its defences on for a week, makes bitter chemicals it does not need, and grows half as much as an unwarned plant. A warning that never ends is a burden.

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** A signal that fades is a signal that can be updated. Evaporation, diffusion and decay are not losses; they are how a message says "now" instead of "once".
