Coal is a swamp forest buried three hundred million years ago before it could rot: photosynthesis banked. Oil and gas are plankton buried under seabeds: the same. Carbon that a leaf lifted out of the air with ancient sunlight, and that decomposers never returned, is a fossil fuel. Burning it is the slow loop's return valve, from VIII.8 L01, opened by hand, a hundred million years of banking spent in two centuries.

The carbon goes to the air as carbon dioxide, faster than leaves, sea and rock can take it back. The air holds more heat. The sea holds more acid. The dials of every biome, from yesterday, shift, and the sieve of selection, from VIII.5, changes faster than most populations can answer it.

The bottle's three sabotages say the same thing at three scales. Remove the returners and the loop jams. Remove the top and the middle booms and crashes. Change the air and every dial moves at once.

**Fossil fuel is ancient photosynthesis, buried unrotted. Burning it returns carbon to the air faster than the loops can take it back, warming the air and souring the sea. Every part of a web, bottom and top, holds the rest up.**
