Copying errors are the only source of new spellings on the tape. Most, from VI.3 L04 and IV.5 L05, change nothing. A few break a protein. A very few change one for the better, in some circumstance that may not have arrived yet.

Zero errors: no new spellings, ever. The population is a clone field, from VIII.1 L04, and dies to the first new key.

Too many: broken proteins pile up faster than they can be weeded out, and every line degrades.

In between: enough variation for some individual to suit whatever comes, few enough that most individuals still work. Proofreading and repair, from VIII.1 L02, are tuned to leave that much, not none.

**A copier that never errs makes a population that cannot change and dies at the first change. One that errs too much collapses. The one in a billion that survives proofreading is not a failure of accuracy; it is the source of every difference this Book runs on.**
