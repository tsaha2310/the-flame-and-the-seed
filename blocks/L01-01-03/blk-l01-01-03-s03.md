Three bins appear when you are done.

The khichdi jar and the air balloon fall apart with gentle tools. Sieve out the dal. Cool the air and one part of it turns to liquid before the rest. Nothing new is made; the parts were only mixed. This is a mixture.

Water, salt and sugar do not fall apart with gentle tools. Evaporate salt water and you get salt back, whole. But pass a current through water and it splits into two gases that are not water at all. Water is made of other things, held together in a fixed way. This is a compound.

The iron nail cannot be split by any tool on the table. Not sieve, not heat, not current. It is one kind of stuff all the way down. This is an element.

The black burned wool goes in the compound bin: iron joined to oxygen, a new substance.

**A mixture can be un-mixed. A compound must be broken. An element cannot be broken at all.**
