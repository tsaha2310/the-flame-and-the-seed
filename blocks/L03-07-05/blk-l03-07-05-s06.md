Sour and slippery are done. Hydrogen ions given or taken. Ten times a step. Cancelled by titration. Oxides on either side of the staircase. And a buffer that leans back.

Ajji drops the ink into the jug one more time. It spreads. It does not return.

"Every reaction in this Book," she says, "runs downhill on the Hill. But the ink did not fall anywhere. It only spread. Why did it happen at all?"

That is the question the next module answers, and it is the deepest one in the course.
