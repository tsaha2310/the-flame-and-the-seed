"Imagine a haystack," says Ajji, "and you want to know if there is a stone in it. You cannot look. What do you do?"

"Throw things at it," says Kabir, instantly.

"Then what?"

"If they all go through, no stone. If one bounces back, stone."

Ajji nods. "Ernest Rutherford, 1909. His haystack was a sheet of gold so thin that light shone through it. His stones were the atoms in the gold. His thrown things were tiny, fast particles with a positive charge."
