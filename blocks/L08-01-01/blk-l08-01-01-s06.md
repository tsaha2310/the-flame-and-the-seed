Kabir's whole-kept world gives heavy-and-light bands nobody has ever seen.

**Predict first:** what is the mechanism, and what does the band pattern look like?

<!-- STEP id="run" -->
**Turn the dial.** A copier would have to read both strands of a closed helix without opening it, which no pairing mechanism can do, and the bands would be heavy plus light. The real bands say no. The sim also shows what whole-kept would mean: one helix in every cell is the original, unchanged for a lifetime, and every error goes into the copies. In the real, semi-conservative world, every helix is half old and errors are shared.

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** The copying mechanism follows from the pairing. Unzip and pair, and half-and-half is the only possible result.
