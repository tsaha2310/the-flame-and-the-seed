> **The Lens says:** The day runs in two minutes. Blood glucose is the line a real meter reads, with the real safe band. The two hormones are dials you turn; in life a gland behind the stomach turns them. The liver, muscle and fat cells respond as in Book V.

Breakfast. The line climbs. Turn hormone B up. Watch the liver deposit and the muscles take glucose in. Watch the line.

Mid-morning, nothing eaten. The line falls. Turn hormone A up. Watch the liver withdraw.

Cricket. The line drops fast. Manage it.

Get through the whole day in the band. Then hand the dials to the pancreas and watch it do the same.
