"A cell makes an amino acid it needs," says Ajji, "through five enzymes in a row. Each hands its product to the next. If the cell makes too much, it wastes coins. If too little, it cannot build protein. Who tells the first enzyme to slow down?"

"The cell does," says Kabir, and then hears himself. "No. Nobody is in charge."

"Nobody is in charge. Run the factory."
