"A small squid in Hawaii," says Ajji, "hunts at night in shallow water under the moon. Its shadow would give it away to fish below. So it glows from its underside, exactly as bright as the moonlight, and casts no shadow."

"Squids glow?"

"Bacteria in a pouch in the squid glow. And a single bacterium never glows. A million together do. Somehow each one knows how many others are there. Grow the colony."
