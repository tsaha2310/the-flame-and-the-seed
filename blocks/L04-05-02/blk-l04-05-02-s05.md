Kabir's order-does-not-matter world has a few hundred proteins in total. Not enough to run a single yeast. He puts the order back.

**Predict first:** how many different proteins can twenty beads make in this world?

<!-- STEP id="run" -->
**Turn the dial.** The count of different chains collapses from an astronomical number to a few hundred, one per recipe of bead counts. The sim previews: with so few shapes, there are not enough different cutters for even the sugars of one seed.

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** The sequence is the information. Same beads in a different order is a different machine, and that is why twenty beads are enough to build everything.
