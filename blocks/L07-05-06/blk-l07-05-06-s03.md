The thermostat: senses heat, yes. Responds, yes. Holds a line, yes, and well. Made its own sensor: no; a factory did. Replaces the sensor when it wears: no; a plumber does. Copies itself: no.

The pancreas cell: senses glucose, with carriers it made from its own tape. Responds, with insulin it made. Holds a line, with a partner cell that pushes the other way. Made its own sensor, yes, and its own relay, from a recipe it carries. Replaces them as they wear, constantly. Copies itself: yes, and that is Book VIII.

The boiled seed: no to all six. The flame: it responds to a draught, and that is all.
