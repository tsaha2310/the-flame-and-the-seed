Two mangoes to Kabir.

Ajji is not troubled. "You know what everything is made of. You know how it is held. You do not yet know what it does."

"Does?"

"Things change. The wool burned. The salt dissolved. The seed grew. Every change is atoms letting go of one grip and taking another. That is a reaction, and the flame and the seed each run one. Book III is about which one."

She opens the fridge. The milk from yesterday has turned. "There," she says. "That is a reaction too. And it is faster on the shelf than in here. Why?"
