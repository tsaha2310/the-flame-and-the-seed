Kabir swaps the two communities and both die in a season.

**Predict first:** what happens in each case in the first year?

<!-- STEP id="run" -->
**Turn the dial.** Thar plants in the Ghats' rain rot at the root and are overgrown by anything that arrives. Ghats trees in the Thar drop every leaf in a month and die of thirst by the second. Neither community is wrong; each is right for its dials and only its dials.

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** Fitted to a place means unfitted to another. That is what selection makes, and why a changed climate is a changed sieve.
