Two opposite charges close together sit low on the Hill. Pulled apart, they sit high. Let go, and they fall toward each other, exactly as the marble fell down the stairs.

Two like charges are the other way round. Pushed together, they sit high. Let go and they fly apart, downhill.

The stored energy is called electrical potential energy. It is the same idea as the marble's height, with one difference. The marble's slope was made by the Earth's pull. This slope is made by the charges themselves.

**Opposite charges near each other are low on the Hill. Anything that lets them get closer is downhill, and happens by itself.**

Keep this sentence. It is the reason a spark jumps, the reason the iron took oxygen, and the reason the seed holds together at all.
