> **The Lens says:** The shop balance is real. The atom balance is imaginary; nobody can weigh one atom on a pan. The atom weights are real, measured in other ways.

Weigh ten grains of rice. Weigh the whole bag. Work out how many grains. Check by counting a spoonful.

Do dal. Do chickpeas.

Now switch to atoms. The sim tells you one carbon weighs twelve times one hydrogen, and one oxygen weighs sixteen times. Weigh a bag of each and count them.
