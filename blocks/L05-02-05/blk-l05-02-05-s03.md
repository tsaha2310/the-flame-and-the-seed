Kabir switches the pumps off and the gut lining bursts cell by cell.

"Everything ran on that one gradient."

"Everything. And the sugar?"

**Predict first:** the salt and sugar are still in the gut; what stops them reaching the blood?

<!-- STEP id="run" -->
**Turn the dial.** With the pump off, sodium inside the cell climbs to match outside. The sodium-glucose carrier, which only works when sodium is falling into the cell, stops; glucose stops. Water follows nothing and stays in the gut. Then the cell itself: sodium, and the water that follows it, pile up inside; the cell swells; the cell bursts. Every gut cell in the sim goes the same way.

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** The pump is upstream of everything. The sodium gradient it makes is what the sugar carrier runs on, what the water follows, and what keeps the cell's own water in check. Switch it off and the cell swells and bursts.
