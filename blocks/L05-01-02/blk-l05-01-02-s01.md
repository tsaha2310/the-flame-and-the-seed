The brass lens goes over the root tip of the new seed. Ajji tilts the ring, and the root hairs fill the glass. Tilt again, and the hairs are made of little boxes, stacked like bricks.

"Boxes," says Kabir.

"Robert Hooke said the same in 1665. He was looking at a slice of cork, and the boxes reminded him of the small rooms monks sleep in. He called them cells. The name stuck to something he never imagined."
