A straight ladder scatters X-rays into a grid. A random coil scatters them into a blur. A spiral scatters them into an X, because the two arms of the X are the two slopes of the spiral seen edge on. Photo 51 was an X. The molecule was a spiral.

The spacing of the bars said how tight the spiral was: one full turn every ten rungs. The dark blobs at top and bottom said how far apart the rungs were. And a missing bar in the pattern said there were two spirals, not one, wound around each other, offset.

The molecule is DNA: deoxyribonucleic acid. Two chains of nucleotides wound round each other into a spiral. The shape is a double helix. James Watson and Francis Crick built the model in 1953 with Franklin's numbers; the photograph had said what shape to build.

This is Rutherford's trick from Book I. Fire something at what you cannot see. Read the pattern of what bounces. Infer the shape. Franklin never saw a helix. She measured one.

**DNA is two chains of nucleotides wound into a double helix. Nobody saw it; an X-ray shadow gave its shape and its dimensions.**
