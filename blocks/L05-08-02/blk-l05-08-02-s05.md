Kabir's yeast-muscle world has drunk cricketers. He decides lactate is the better deal.

**Predict first:** what would a sprint produce?

<!-- STEP id="run" -->
**Turn the dial.** The muscle makes ethanol and carbon dioxide. The carbon dioxide has to be carried off by blood that is already busy; the ethanol enters the blood, and after a hard match a player is drunk. The liver cannot turn ethanol back into glucose; the carbon that left as gas is gone.

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** Lactate keeps all three carbons, so the liver can rebuild glucose from it. Animals chose the recoverable path. Yeast chose the other, and we make bread and wine from the choice.
