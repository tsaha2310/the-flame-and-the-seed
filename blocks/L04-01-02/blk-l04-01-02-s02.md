> **The Lens says:** Atoms as balls, bonds as sticks, at a hundred million times. Neither is real. The bench enforces valency: carbon takes four bonds, hydrogen one. Bond strength readouts are real averages.

Make methane. Then join two carbons and fill with hydrogens. Then three. Then ten in a row.

Take the ten-chain and pull one carbon out of line to make a branch. Then bend a six-chain round and join the ends.

Make two carbons share two pairs. Then three.

For each thing you build, read the weakest bond.
