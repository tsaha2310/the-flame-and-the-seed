The electron can only sit on certain rungs. Not between them. The rungs are energy levels, and the old name for them is shells.

A knock lifts the electron to a higher rung. It falls back to a lower one. The fall is a fixed height, so it releases a fixed amount of energy, and that energy comes out as one packet of light. A packet of light is a photon.

The colour of a photon is its energy. Red photons carry the least. Blue carry more. Violet the most you can see. A bigger fall gives a bluer photon.

Sodium's rungs are spaced so that its easiest fall gives a yellow photon. Every sodium atom has the same rungs, so every sodium atom gives the same yellow. That is the fingerprint.

**Electrons can only sit at certain energies. A fall between two of them gives one photon of one exact colour.**

Now the honesty card, said plainly. Electrons do not circle. A shell is an energy level. The cloud shows where the electron is likely to be. The rings in the drawings are only a way to count.
