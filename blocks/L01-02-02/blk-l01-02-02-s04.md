The heavy marble fell. Its potential energy became kinetic energy, and the thread passed that on to the light marble, which climbed. Pushing a thing through a distance is called doing work. The heavy marble did work on the light one.

With no rubbing, the light marble climbed exactly as much as the fall was worth. With rubbing, it climbed less, and the track got a little warmer. Add the climb and the warmth together and you get exactly the fall. Every time.

This is conservation of energy. Energy is never made and never destroyed. It only changes form: height, speed, warmth.

From here on, this picture gets a name: the Hill. A ball on a landscape. Downhill happens by itself. A hump blocks the way, even when the far side is lower. One fall can lift another ball.

**Nothing in this course ever runs uphill on its own. If something goes up, something else came down.**
