> **The Lens says:** Molecules are drawn bent, with hydrogen bonds as dotted lines. Real bonds are pulls, not lines. Time is slowed a trillion times when you watch molecules, and sped up ten thousand times when you watch the pond.

Start with liquid water at 20 degrees. Count the molecules in the box.

Cool it slowly. Watch the hydrogen bonds. At zero, watch what they do. Count the molecules in the box again.

Rotate the frozen box. Look at the shape the molecules have locked into. Find the holes.
