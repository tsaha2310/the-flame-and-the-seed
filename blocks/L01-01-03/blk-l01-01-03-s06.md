Every element you have found tonight is on a chart Ajji unrolls on the wall. About ninety kinds of stuff that cannot be broken. Everything else in the kitchen, and in the seed, and in the flame, is made by joining them.

"How many elements are in a moong seed?" you ask.

"Burn one and see what is left," says Ajji.

Kabir looks at the sprouting bowl in alarm. "Not those ones."
