> **The Lens says:** The objects are real-sized. The charges on them are drawn as small plus and minus marks; in life you cannot see charge at all. The threads are stiffer than real thread.

Six things hang on threads. Some have been rubbed. You can bring any two close and watch them swing toward each other or away.

Work out:

- how many kinds of "rubbed" there are
- which kinds push and which pull
- what an unrubbed object does

Write your rule before you turn to the next slide.
