Rain again, so the stairs are the playground. Kabir has built a marble run out of Ajji's cardboard boxes and a length of curtain rail.

He lets a marble go from the top step. It races down, shoots up a little ramp he has made, and stops halfway up. It rolls back. Up the first slope, not quite to the top. Back. Each time a bit less, until it sits in the dip between.

"It is tired," says Kabir.

"Marbles do not get tired," says Ajji, "but something is being spent. Find out what."
