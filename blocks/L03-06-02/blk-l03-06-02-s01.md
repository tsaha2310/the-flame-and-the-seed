Petrol in a can does not burn. Petrol in a can with a lit match near it burns all at once.

Wood in a pile does not burn. Wood in a pile with a burning twig under it burns for an hour.

"In both cases," says Ajji, "the burning is downhill. Fuel and oxygen are high, ash and gas are low. So why does it wait for the match?"
