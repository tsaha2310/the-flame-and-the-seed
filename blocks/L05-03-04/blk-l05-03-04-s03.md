Kabir names shrinking and stuffing. He does not name the third.

"The nerve," says Ajji. "Every message a nerve sends is a bubble spat out. Hold that until VII.2."

**Predict first:** three things fail; name them before you run.

<!-- STEP id="run" -->
**Turn the dial.** First, the cell shrinks, because wall goes in and never comes out; in half an hour it has eaten its own surface. Second, everything the cell made to send out piles up in vesicles inside: cutters, hormones, fence. Nothing reaches the outside. Third, the nerve panel: the bubbles at the nerve's tip cannot fuse with its wall, so nothing is released to the muscle, and the muscle never moves. The sim's label appears at the end: this is what the botulism toxin does, and why it stops breathing.

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** Exocytosis is how a cell gives anything to anything else. Block it and the cell is mute, shrinking, and stuffed. The nerve panel is Book VII's whole mechanism, seen from the wall.
