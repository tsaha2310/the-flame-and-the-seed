Kabir's sterile-soil moong is a pale, poor plant. He adds the bacteria and it greens.

**Predict first:** how do the two rows compare after eight weeks?

<!-- STEP id="run" -->
**Turn the dial.** The sterile row is small and yellow, short of nitrogen, with few pods. The row with rhizobia is dark green and heavy with pods. Add nitrogen fertiliser to the sterile row and it catches up, at a cost in fertiliser that the other row paid in sugar.

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** The partnership is worth about as much as a sack of fertiliser per field. It is not a curiosity; it is how a legume lives.
