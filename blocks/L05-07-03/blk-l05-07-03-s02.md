Measure all four pullers. Order them from weakest to strongest.

Put NADH at the weak end and oxygen at the strong end. Feed one NADH. Watch the electrons hop from block to block. Read the volts of each hop.

Watch the blocks as the electrons pass. Something crosses the wall at three of them. Count it.

Feed the other kind of full carrier. Watch where it joins the line.
