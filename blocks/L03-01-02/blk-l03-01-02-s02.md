> **The Lens says:** Molecules as tiles with their atoms drawn as balls. The pans weigh atoms by count, not by mass; a real balance weighs mass, but the two agree because every atom of a kind weighs the same.

Put H2 and O2 tiles on the left pan and an H2O tile on the right. Read the counts under each pan.

Add tiles, only whole ones, until every element's count is the same on both sides. You cannot cut a tile.

Then do the same for: carbon burning, methane burning, the fizz from last night, iron rusting, and the electrolysis of water.
