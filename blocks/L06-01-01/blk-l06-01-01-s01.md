"Who decides the order of the beads?" says Ajji. "Kabir's question. In 1928 an English doctor named Fred Griffith was not asking it. He was trying to make a vaccine against pneumonia, and he found something that made no sense."

"What?"

"He killed a deadly germ with heat, mixed the dead germ with a harmless live one, and the harmless one became deadly. Something passed from the dead to the living. Guess what it could have been, then run it."
