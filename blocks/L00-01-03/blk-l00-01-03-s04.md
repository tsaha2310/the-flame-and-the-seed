Ten seconds in boiling water is enough. The seed never sprouts. Not late, not small. Never.

Yet nothing has been taken out of it. Weigh it: same weight. Look with the lens: same coat, same two halves, same tiny curled shoot inside. Whatever the boiling water did, it did without removing a thing.

For a very long time, people explained this with a single idea. Living things carry something extra, a spark or a force that non-living things lack. Boiling drives it out. The idea had a name: vital force.

Notice what kind of idea it is. It is a hypothesis, like spontaneous generation was. It can be tested. Nobody has tested it yet in this course, so it stays on the board.

**The boiled seed keeps every part and still cannot sprout. Something about how the parts were arranged has been lost.**

That sentence is not an answer. It is the shape of the whole investigation.
