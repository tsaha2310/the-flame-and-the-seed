Ajji has drawn the battery a chemist named Daniell built in 1836. Two beakers. Zinc in zinc sulphate on the left. Copper in copper sulphate on the right. A wire between the metals, and a paper strip soaked in salt water joining the two liquids.

"Why two beakers?" says Kabir. "One was enough yesterday."

"One was enough for a minute. Watch what happens in one beaker after an hour."
