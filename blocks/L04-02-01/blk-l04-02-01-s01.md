The red cylinder under the counter. Kabir has been told never to touch the valve, and never has, and is very curious about what is inside.

"Carbon and hydrogen," says Ajji. "Nothing else. Chains of three and four carbons, squeezed until they are a liquid. Open the valve and they come out as gas."

"Just carbon and hydrogen? Like the candle?"

"The candle is chains of twenty or thirty. Same family. Longer."
