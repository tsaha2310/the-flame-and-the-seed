Atoms are never made or lost, from Book I. A carbon atom moves between molecules, and the moves are the reactions of this course.

In the air it is carbon dioxide, the bottom of the Hill, from IV.2 L04. A leaf lifts it into sugar, from V.10, paying with light. The sugar becomes starch in a seed, from IV.4. Kabir eats the seed; the starch is cut to glucose, from V.4; the glucose walks down the staircase, from V.6 and V.7; and the carbon leaves in his breath as carbon dioxide again, from V.7 L06. A few days, from air to air.

Or the carbon dioxide dissolves in the sea, from III.5, and a shellfish builds it into a shell, and the shell sinks and becomes limestone, and stays for a hundred million years, until a volcano cooks the rock and returns the carbon to the air. Or a swamp fern takes it, dies, and is buried before it rots, and becomes coal, and stays three hundred million years until someone digs it up and burns it.

The whole set of paths, the fast loop through leaves and breath and the slow loop through shells and coal, is the carbon cycle. Every carbon atom in you has been through it many times, and will go round again.

**The carbon cycle: carbon dioxide in the air is lifted into sugar by leaves, passes through everything that eats, and returns as breath and smoke; or it sinks into shells and coal for millions of years and returns by volcano and fire. The atoms never change; only their molecules do.**
