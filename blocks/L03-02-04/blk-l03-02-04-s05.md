Kabir seals a seed in a jar in the sim. It stops. He opens the jar. It runs, slowly, until the store runs out.

"Unless it makes more sugar," he says. "How does it make more sugar?"

"Book V," says Ajji.

**Predict first:** what is the limiting reagent for a seed in a sealed jar?

<!-- STEP id="run" -->
**Turn the dial.** In a sealed jar, oxygen runs out and the seed's burn stops, though sugar remains. With the jar open, sugar is the limit, and the seed stops when its store is gone, unless it makes more. The sim leaves that last clause dangling on purpose.

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** The limiting reagent for a flame is usually fuel. For a seed in a jar it is oxygen. For a seedling in the open it is something you meet in Book V.
