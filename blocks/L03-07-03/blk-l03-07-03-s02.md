> **The Lens says:** The burette, flask and indicator behave like the real thing. The cut-away shows hydrogen ions and hydroxide ions meeting and becoming water, magnified a hundred million times. Drops are real-sized.

The flask holds 20 ml of acid of unknown strength with a few drops of indicator. The burette holds base of known strength.

Add base ten drops at a time, watching the colour. When it flickers, switch to one drop at a time. Stop on the drop that makes the colour hold.

Read the burette. Use the recipe, one hydroxide per hydrogen, to work out the acid's concentration.

Do it three times. Your readings should agree within a drop.
