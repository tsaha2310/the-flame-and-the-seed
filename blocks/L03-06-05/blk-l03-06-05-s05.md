Kabir's fixed-split world cannot make a fizzy drink or run a seed. He gives it up.

**Predict first:** in this world, can you ever drive a reaction to completion by removing product?

<!-- STEP id="run" -->
**Turn the dial.** Remove product and nothing replaces it. Add reactant and it piles up unused. The fizzy drink cannot be made by squeezing gas in. And the sim shows a preview: a pipeline of reactions in a seed, each feeding the next, jams at the first step because nothing pulls it along.

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** Living chemistry works by pulling products away so that each step keeps running. Le Chatelier is the reason a chain of small steps can go all the way downhill.
