Kabir is pleased with himself. "So the flame is alive."

Ajji does not answer. She never does. She slides two cards to the middle.

The mule. A living animal that cannot have babies. The seven-signs list says it fails "reproduce".

The virus. It reproduces by the million, once it is inside a living thing. Outside one it is a crystal. It does not eat, breathe, grow or move.

"You have two choices," she says. "Say the mule is not alive. Or say the list is not the answer."

Nobody says the mule is not alive.
