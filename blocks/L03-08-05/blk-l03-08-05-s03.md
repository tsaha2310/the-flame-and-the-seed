Ink gathering into a drop. A shattered glass leaping back onto the table. A candle un-burning, smoke sucking back into a growing wick. Every reversed clip shows free energy rising by itself, ways decreasing, heat gathering back from the room. You have never seen any of them, and you never will.

The two clips you could not tell are a ball rolling on a smooth floor and a pendulum swinging. Nearly nothing spreads out in them, so they look the same both ways. Those are the exceptions that show the rule: the arrow of time is spreading out.

Left alone, everything slides down the free-energy Hill to its valley floor and stays there. Heat evens out. Ink evens out. Differences flatten. Fuel burns. Structure loosens into the most-ways arrangement it can find. This is the Levelling. It has no villain and no purpose. It is only counting, and counting always wins in the end.

The boiled seed is at its valley floor. Its molecules are all still there, but they have slid into the most-ways arrangement, the tangled one, and there is nothing in it that is being held away from equilibrium. It is level.

The flame is a very fast slide toward the floor. It is the Levelling happening in front of you, at a thousand degrees.

The live seed is not at the floor. It holds differences: more of some things inside than out, molecules folded into shapes that are not the most-ways shapes, sugar unburned in the presence of oxygen. It sits above the valley, and it stays there. That takes constant coupling, constant falls paying for lifts.

**Left alone, everything levels: down the Hill to the valley floor. A living thing is something that stays above the floor, and the boiled seed has already reached it.**
