"Where would the seed be?" you ask.

**Predict first:** in a world where like charges attract and opposites repel, where do all the charges end up?

<!-- STEP id="run" -->
**Turn the dial.** Every positive charge rushes toward every other positive, every negative toward every negative. Two lumps form, then the sim zooms out: the lumps keep growing, with nothing to stop them. There is no spacing, no structure, nothing small.

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** The two-kind rule is what makes atoms possible at all. Things spread out because like charges push apart, and things hold together because opposites pull. Both are needed.
