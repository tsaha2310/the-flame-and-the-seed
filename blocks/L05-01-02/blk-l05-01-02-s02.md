> **The Lens says:** The two microscopes are drawn as they were; Hooke's was a tube of lenses, Leeuwenhoek's a single bead of glass held to the eye. What they saw is drawn from their own sketches. The moong root is what the brass Lens shows today.

Look at cork through Hooke's tube. Sketch what you see.

Look at pond water through Leeuwenhoek's bead. Watch. Look at tooth scrapings. Watch.

Now the Lens on the moong root. Find the boxes. Zoom on one. Then a drop of blood: find the discs.
