> **The Lens says:** On the left, Mendel's crosses with their factor letters; on the right, chromosomes in meiosis with the same letters drawn at their places. Fertilisation is drawn as two nuclei fusing; a real egg and pollen tube are more complicated.

Tt crossed with Tt. Watch the T chromosome and the t chromosome separate in meiosis. List each parent's gametes. Fertilise at random. Count. Compare to three to one.

TtRr crossed with TtRr, with T and R on different pairs. Watch the two pairs line up independently. List gametes. Fertilise. Compare to nine, three, three, one.

Now put T and R on the same pair, close together. Watch. Count. Then far apart, with crossing over on. Count.
