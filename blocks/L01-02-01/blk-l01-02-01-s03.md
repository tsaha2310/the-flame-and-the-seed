You cannot do it. A marble released from a height never climbs above that height. It can climb exactly back to it, if nothing rubs, and no higher.

What pushes it down the slope is a force: a push or a pull. Here it is the pull of the Earth.

Sitting high on the track, the still marble has something stored. Let it go and the stored thing turns into speed. The stored thing is potential energy. The speed thing is kinetic energy.

Down the slope: potential turns into kinetic. Up the next slope: kinetic turns back into potential. When the marble stops at the top of a climb, all of it is potential again, and the climb is exactly as high as the fall was.

**A marble can only climb as high as it fell. Energy changes form, but the amount it started with is a ceiling it cannot break.**
