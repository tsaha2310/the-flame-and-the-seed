**Turn the dial.** The toxin sits in each sodium channel's mouth, from V.4's inhibitors, and no gate can open. The poke makes a bump that fades. No wave, anywhere. No myelin: the wave crawls at a metre a second instead of fifty, and the reflex takes a second; the hand burns. The race: the wire carries its signal a metre in a few billionths of a second. The best nerve takes twenty thousandths. A wire moves electrons that are already in it; a nerve opens gates, one after another, each taking a millisecond, and spits molecules across gaps that must diffuse.

**What this nails:** A nerve is a chain of chemical relays, not a wire. Each part, gate, lockout, sleeve, synapse, is a molecule that a toxin or a disease can block. And the chain is slow because every link is a shape changing.
