**Turn the dial.** With overlapping words, changing one letter changes three beads at once, and the next letter is locked by the last: after any given bead, only a few beads are possible. Most sequences cannot be written. The sim's protein bin is mostly empty.

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** The real code is read three letters at a time with no overlap, which lets any bead follow any bead. Overlap was a serious proposal in the 1950s and it fails on this count.
