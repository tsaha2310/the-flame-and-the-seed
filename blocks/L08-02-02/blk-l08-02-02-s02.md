> **The Lens says:** Factors drawn as letters in pairs; the square is the tool Reginald Punnett drew in 1905 to lay out Mendel's logic. The garden check is the sim from yesterday.

Pure tall is TT. Pure short is tt. Cross them: write T and T along the top, t and t down the side, fill the four boxes. Read the children.

Cross two of those children, Tt and Tt. Fill the square. Count the boxes by what they show. Check against the garden.

Cross Tt with tt. Predict. Check. Cross TT with Tt. Predict. Check.

Six crosses right and you have Mendel's first law.
