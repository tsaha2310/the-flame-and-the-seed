Chloroplast, thylakoid, chlorophyll. Red and blue in, green out, an electron lifted.

"One lifted electron," says Kabir. "Where does it go? If it falls back it just makes heat, like the flame's yellow."

"It is caught," says Ajji, "and passed down a staircase you have seen before. And the hole it leaves behind is filled from water, and that is where the oxygen comes from."
