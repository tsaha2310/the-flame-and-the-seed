Kabir's no-water-loss fat is a pile of pieces that do not hold. He notes that his own lunch would be undigestible.

**Predict first:** can a fat be built, and can it be taken apart?

<!-- STEP id="run" -->
**Turn the dial.** The groups stick, but no ester forms; it is a loose, wobbly join with no real bond, and the "fat" falls apart when jiggled. And it cannot be taken apart cleanly either, because there is no water to add back. The sim's seed cannot store fat, and cannot digest it.

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** Losing water is how a real bond forms between the pieces, and adding water is how it is undone. The seed's whole chemistry of building and breaking uses this one move.
