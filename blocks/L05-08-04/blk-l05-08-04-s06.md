**Turn the dial.** The open flask now gulps sugar like the sealed one, pours out alcohol into the air, and grows poorly. A microbe that cannot use oxygen when it has it is wasting fifteen coins per glucose. Beside it, a competitor that can switch grows ten times as fast and takes the flask.

**What this nails:** The switch is worth a lot. Le Chatelier from III.6 says why the powerhouse pulls the pipeline when oxygen is present: oxygen empties buckets faster than pyruvate can, and the electrons go where the pull is.
