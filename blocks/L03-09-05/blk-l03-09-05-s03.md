**Turn the dial.** With the motor, most of the fall becomes lifted weight and a little becomes heat. With the bare wire, every bit becomes heat, and the zinc is used faster, because nothing slows the flow. The work-done readout is zero. Then the diya panel: fuel giving electrons, oxygen taking them, and no wire, no motor, no load anywhere. Every bit of the fall becomes heat and light. Work done: zero.

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** A flame is a short circuit. The same chemistry as a battery, but the fall goes straight to heat with nothing to catch it. That is the first real difference between the two columns.
