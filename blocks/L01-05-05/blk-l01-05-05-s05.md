Kabir has won the first mango. The flame and the seed are made of the same stuff.

Ajji does not seem disappointed. "You have the right elements. Now find out how they are held together. A pile of bricks is not a house."

"Maybe the seed's atoms are held together differently," you say.

"Maybe," says Ajji. "Salt shatters. Copper dents. Sugar dissolves and does not conduct. Three different grips, and you have not seen any of them yet."

Book II opens with a hammer.
