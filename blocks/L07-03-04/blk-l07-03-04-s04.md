A threshold of one is no quorum at all: each cell acts as if alone, and pays for nothing worth having. The threshold exists because the payoff needs numbers.

A cheater is a cell that reads the signal and skips the cost. It out-divides its honest neighbours until the shared slime fails and all of them lose. Real biofilms hold cheaters at a low share by tricks Book VIII covers: the slime helps the cells nearest its maker most, so honest cells get more of their own payment back.

A jammer is a molecule shaped for the switch's pocket that fits without bending: an inhibitor, from V.4 L03. Deaf cells never form a biofilm and stay easy to kill. It attacks the conversation rather than the cell, so it may push less hard for resistance than an antibiotic does, though a one-letter change in the pocket can still defeat it. This is real, unfinished research.

**A quorum needs a real threshold and honest payers. Cheaters win short and lose long. A jammer that deafens the switch is a medicine being tested, and mutation is its opponent.**
