> **The Lens says:** Burns are sped up a hundred times. The gas that joins the metal is drawn blue; real oxygen has no colour. The balance is a modern one.

Pick a side.

If you pick phlogiston, the Lens shows the prediction: the metal loses fire-stuff, so it must get lighter.

If you pick oxygen, the prediction is: something from the air joins the metal, so it must get heavier, and the air in a sealed jar must lose exactly that much.

Now run the experiment Antoine Lavoisier ran in Paris in 1774. Heat tin in a sealed glass jar on a balance. Weigh the jar before and after. Open the jar and listen for air rushing in. Weigh the tin alone.

Try it with all three metals.
