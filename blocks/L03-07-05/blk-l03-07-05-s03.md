**Predict first:** what does one glass of nimbu pani do to the pH of unbuffered blood?

<!-- STEP id="run" -->
**Turn the dial.** PH drops from 7.4 to about 6, past the coma line, past the death line. One glass. With the buffer back on, the same glass moves it by less than a tenth. The cut-away shows why: with the buffer, the nimbu's hydrogen ions are grabbed by bicarbonate ions and turned into carbonic acid, which the lungs breathe out as carbon dioxide.

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** A body's steady pH is not because it avoids acid. It is because it holds a weak acid and its partner in reserve, and Le Chatelier does the rest.
