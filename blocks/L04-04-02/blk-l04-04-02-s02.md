> **The Lens says:** Glucose, fructose and galactose drawn as rings with their hydroxyls sticking out. A link forms only where two hydroxyls meet and a water leaves. The cutter from Book V is not drawn; you are doing its job by hand.

Take two glucoses. Bring a hydroxyl on the first ring to a hydroxyl on the second. Join. Catch what leaves. That is malt sugar.

Take a glucose and a fructose. Join. Table sugar.

Take a glucose and a galactose. Join. Milk sugar.

Now take table sugar and add one water molecule back at the link. Watch.
