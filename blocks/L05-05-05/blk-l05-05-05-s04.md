Two lessons from two sabotages.

The bank: withdrawal speed is set by ends. A branched store has hundreds of doors and empties fast; an unbranched one has two and empties slowly. The branching enzyme's coins bought speed. A body that could not branch would deposit fine and be unable to sprint. Book VIII meets a pea that cannot branch, and wrinkles because of it.

The muscle: ATP does two jobs in the myosin cycle. It pays for the pull, and it pays for letting go. Cut the supply and the second job stops first, because a head that has pulled stays bound until a coin releases it. The muscle locks. Rigor after death is every myosin head in the body holding on with no coin to open its hand.

**A store with few ends pays out slowly. A muscle with no ATP does not relax; it locks, because letting go costs a coin too. Rigor is a body out of coins.**
