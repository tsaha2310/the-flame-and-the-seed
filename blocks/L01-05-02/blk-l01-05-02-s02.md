> **The Lens says:** Shells are drawn as rings. Electrons do not circle; a shell is an energy level, and the ring is only a way to count. The nucleus is a dot with a number on it.

Take hydrogen: one proton, one electron. It goes in the innermost shell.

Helium: two protons, two electrons. Both in the inner shell, and it is now full. The inner shell holds two and no more.

Lithium: three. The third has to go into the next shell out.

Keep going to twenty. The second shell holds eight. Then the third starts.

Watch the table beside you. Each time you finish an element, note how many electrons are in its outermost shell, and see which column it lands in.
