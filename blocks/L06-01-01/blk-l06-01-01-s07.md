Transformation. A recipe passing from the dead to the living, and surviving heat.

"Oswald Avery," says Ajji, "spent a decade purifying the transforming stuff. He took away the proteins: still worked. Took away the fats and sugars: still worked. Added an enzyme that cuts DNA: stopped working. He published in 1944 and half the world did not believe him, because everyone knew DNA was a boring molecule with only four letters."

"Four letters can spell anything," says Kabir, who did IV.7.

"Tell that to 1944. Tomorrow, the experiment that settled it, with the labelled atoms from Book I."
