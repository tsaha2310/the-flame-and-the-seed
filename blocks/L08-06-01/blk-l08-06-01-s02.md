> **The Lens says:** Cards show real features: fur, feathers, scales, backbone, four limbs, milk, eggs, flowers, seeds, and so on. The tree you build is a hypothesis; the sim scores it against how many features each grouping explains. Trick cards are marked only after you place them.

Take the animal cards. Group first by backbone. Then, within backbones, by four limbs. Then by fur, feathers, scales. Nest the groups.

Place the whale. Place the bat. Read the conflicts. Move each until the conflicts drop.

Place the platypus. Read.

Do the same for plants: flowers, seeds, no seeds. Place the cactus.
