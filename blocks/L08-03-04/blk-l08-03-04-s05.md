Kabir removes the checkpoint and most of his seeds fail. He restores it.

**Predict first:** what fraction of gametes carry the wrong count?

<!-- STEP id="run" -->
**Turn the dial.** With no checkpoint, about a third of gametes have a pair doubled or missing. Most fertilisations fail early. Of those that develop, many carry three of some chromosome. The population's birth rate collapses. In the real body, the checkpoint holds nondisjunction to a few percent, rising with a mother's age as the egg's held-open line-up loosens.

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** The line-up checkpoint from mitosis is even more important in meiosis, where the error is passed to every cell of a child. Its slow loosening in eggs held for decades is why some conditions rise with the mother's age.
