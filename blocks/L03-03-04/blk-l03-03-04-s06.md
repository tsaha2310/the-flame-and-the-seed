Redox is done. Losing and gaining, scored on the Tug-of-War, with thieves and givers.

Carbon's score runs from minus 4 to plus 4. A fuel is carbon with a low score. Burning takes it to the top. The distance it falls is what heats the pot.

"How much?" says Kabir.

"That," says Ajji, "needs a cup of water and a cup of oil, and the same flame under each."
