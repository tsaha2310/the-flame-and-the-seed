> **The Lens says:** The atom is drawn as a bright ball on a map of the verandah, the garden, the town, the sea and the rock beneath. Each move is a real reaction from earlier Books. Times are shown, from seconds to millions of years.

Start in the smoke. Move to the air. Read the molecule.

Into a moong leaf. Watch RuBisCO take it. Read the molecule. Into a seed as starch. Into Kabir at dinner. Out in his breath. Read the times.

Into the sea. Into a shell. Into rock on the seabed. Read the time held.

Back through a volcano. Into a fern three hundred million years ago. Into coal. Into a power station. Back to the air.

Visit every place. Score.
