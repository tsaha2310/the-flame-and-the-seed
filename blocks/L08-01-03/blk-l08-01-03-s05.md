Kabir skips the line-up and his daughters are lopsided. He restores it and the counts come out right, five runs in a row.

**Predict first:** what do the daughters get?

<!-- STEP id="run" -->
**Turn the dial.** Chromosomes near one end go to that end, both copies. One daughter gets three of one chromosome and none of another. Over many divisions, the sim's tissue fills with cells missing genes or carrying extras, and most die; some, with extras of the wrong genes, divide without stopping, from VII.5 L05.

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** The line-up is a checkpoint. The cell waits until every chromosome is attached to threads from both ends before cutting the joins. Skipping it is how cancers often lose or gain chromosomes.
