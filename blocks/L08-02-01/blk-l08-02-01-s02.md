> **The Lens says:** Pea plants drawn with seven visible traits, as Mendel chose them: seed shape, seed colour, flower colour, pod shape, pod colour, flower position, height. Crosses are done by hand as he did them, with a brush. Generations pass in seconds. Counts are real ratios with real scatter.

Take a tall plant and a short plant that each breed true. Cross them. Count the children's heights.

Cross those children with each other. Count the grandchildren.

Do the same for round versus wrinkled seeds. For yellow versus green. For purple versus white flowers.

Try each with ten plants per cross, then with a thousand. Write the ratios.
