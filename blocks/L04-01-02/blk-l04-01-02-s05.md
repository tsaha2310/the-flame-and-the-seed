Kabir weakens the carbon bond. His ten-chain falls into three pieces. He tries to make a ring and it opens like a bracelet with a broken clasp.

**Predict first:** how long a chain can you build before it breaks?

<!-- STEP id="run" -->
**Turn the dial.** Chains of three or four hold; longer ones snap at room temperature from ordinary jiggling. Rings pop open. The bench cannot make anything the size of a sugar, let alone a chain of them.

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** The strength of the carbon-carbon bond is what makes long molecules possible. Silicon's bond to itself is exactly this weak, and tomorrow you find out what that costs.
