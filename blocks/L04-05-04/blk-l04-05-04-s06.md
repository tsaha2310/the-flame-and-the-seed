**Turn the dial.** With a rigid pocket, only a perfect match fits, and even a perfect match rattles. With the real, slightly flexible pocket, the fit tightens as the molecule enters: the fold bends a little to grip it. The sim shows both, side by side, and the flexible one holds.

**What this nails:** A fit can bend the piece. That is why Lock and Shape is not "lock and key". The bending is what a cutter uses in Book V, and what a signal uses in Book VII.
