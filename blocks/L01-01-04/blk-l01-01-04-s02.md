> **The Lens says:** Gases are drawn as coloured balls, hydrogen white and oxygen red. Real gases have no colour and their pieces are far too small to draw. The balance is honest.

Take any amount of hydrogen and any amount of oxygen. Combine them. Weigh the water you get, and weigh what is left over.

Find the recipe that leaves nothing over. Then try to make water with a different recipe. Then switch the sim to counting mode and see the balls join.
