Kabir's fenceless plant cell bursts in the rain. He understands why moong seeds need a coat.

**Predict first:** what does the plant cell do in pure water without its fence?

<!-- STEP id="run" -->
**Turn the dial.** It swells like the blood cell and bursts. A plant in the rain, with no fences, would dissolve. Then the reverse: a blood cell given a fence in salt water holds its shape but its skin still shrinks away inside; the fence does not stop plasmolysis, only bursting.

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** The fence is a pressure vessel. It lets plant cells sit in fresh water without bursting, and lets them use that pressure to stand up. Animals never grew fences, and keep their blood at exactly the saltiness of their cells instead.
