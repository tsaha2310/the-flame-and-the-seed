Morning. The power is back. Kabir has already checked the bowls.

"They look the same," he reports. Both lots of seeds have swollen. Both are pale and wrinkled. He is right, and you write it down.

At breakfast the milk has gone off. Ajji sniffs it and pours it into the dahi pot instead. "Something got in overnight," she says.

"What got in?" says Kabir. "The lid was on."

"That question," says Ajji, "took two thousand years to answer."
