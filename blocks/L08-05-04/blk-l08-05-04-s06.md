Kabir's unseparated finches stay one species with a spread of beaks. When he makes them choosy about mates, they begin to split.

**Predict first:** do two species form?

<!-- STEP id="run" -->
**Turn the dial.** Big-beaked and small-beaked finches are both favoured, but they keep breeding with each other, and every generation mixes the alleles back. Beaks spread into a range and no split forms. Only when big-beaked birds start choosing big-beaked mates, which the sim lets you switch on, do two groups begin to pull apart on one island.

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** Separation, by sea or by choice of mate, is what lets differences pile up instead of mixing away. Selection alone, with free interbreeding, makes variety, not species.
