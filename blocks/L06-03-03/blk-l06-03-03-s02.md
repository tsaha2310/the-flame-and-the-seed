> **The Lens says:** The mRNA is a real gene's message, letters drawn. The table is the real code. Reading three at a time from the right place is your job; in the cell a machine does it, and that machine is tomorrow.

Find where the message begins. There is a codon that always marks it. Read forward from there, three letters at a time.

Look up each codon. Add the bead. Keep going until you hit a codon that names no bead.

Read all three messages. Then take one message and change one letter in a codon's third position. Read it again.
