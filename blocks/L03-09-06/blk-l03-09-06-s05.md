Three mangoes. Kabir has stopped keeping count.

"You have the same atoms, the same grips, and the same reaction," says Ajji. "You have one clue: there is a load somewhere in the seed. To find it you have to see what the seed is actually made of. Not the elements. The molecules."

She unrolls a new chart on the wall. It is a ladder, and its rungs are labelled with elements: carbon, hydrogen, oxygen, nitrogen, sulphur, phosphorus.

"Book IV," she says. "We climb it one rung at a time. And the first rung was climbed by a man who made something that only a kidney was supposed to make."
