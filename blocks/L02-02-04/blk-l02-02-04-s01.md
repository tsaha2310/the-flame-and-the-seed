Kabir has a matchbox, some matchsticks and a lump of Ajji's atta.

"Make carbon," says Ajji. "One atta ball, four matchsticks, pointing at four corners."

He does. It looks like a tiny jack.

"Now make another and join them, stick to stick."

He does. Now he has two balls and six free sticks. "Can I keep going?"

"That," says Ajji, "is the question Book IV is about. Yes. Forever."
