A big glass jar on the verandah. Ajji has put in soil, a little water, three moong seedlings, some grass, a few snails, a beetle, and a spoon of compost from the heap.

"Sealed," she says. "Nothing in or out but light. In Book V you kept a plant and a snail alive with a lamp. This time there are more parts, and one of them you cannot see. A hundred days."
