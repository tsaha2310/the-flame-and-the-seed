Kabir's any-join world makes a different sugar every time. His table sugar is a random lump. The sim's ants will not touch it.

**Predict first:** can you still make a specific sugar?

<!-- STEP id="run" -->
**Turn the dial.** Rings stick together at random spots in random orientations. No two "sucrose" molecules are the same. Nothing can be cut cleanly, because there is no water to put back. The sim's tongue cannot recognise any of them as sweet.

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** Joining only at hydroxyls, only by losing water, is what makes every sucrose molecule the same sucrose, and what lets a cutter take it apart the same way every time.
