Kabir's base-to-base chain is a ribbon nobody can read. He puts the phosphates back in the spine.

**Predict first:** what does the chain look like, and can it be read?

<!-- STEP id="run" -->
**Turn the dial.** The bases, joined edge to edge, form a stiff flat ribbon with nothing sticking out. The joins block the very groups on the bases that are meant to face outward. There is no free edge to read. And the ribbon has no direction, so no way to know where a message starts.

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** The backbone is a spine so that the bases can be teeth. The information has to hang off something uniform to be readable.
