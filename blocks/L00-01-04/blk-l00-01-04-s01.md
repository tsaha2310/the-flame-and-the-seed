The lens is heavier than a lens should be. Brass, with a ring of tiny marks around the edge that could be numbers.

"It was my grandmother's," Ajji says. "It does not work like the one in your geometry box."

You hold it over the sprouting seed. The root tip jumps to the size of your thumb. Fine hairs on it, like fur.

You tilt the ring. The root tip fills the whole glass. Then the hairs are as thick as ropes. Then the ropes are made of bricks. Then the glass goes dark.

"It only shows what you have the words for," Ajji says. "Learn the words and it goes further."
