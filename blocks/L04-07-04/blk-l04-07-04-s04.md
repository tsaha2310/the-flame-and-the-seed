A message is a choice among possibilities. AGCU says something because it could have been UCGA, or AAAA, or any of 256 four-letter tapes, and it is this one. The more possibilities there were, the more the choice says.

That is what information is: a particular arrangement chosen out of many possible ones. The counting is the same counting as III.8's entropy, turned around. Entropy counts how many arrangements look the same. Information counts how many arrangements could have been written and picks one.

A tape of one letter has one possible arrangement per length. It carries no information beyond its length. A tape of four letters, a hundred long, has four to the hundredth possible arrangements, and one of them is written. That one can specify, say, the order of beads in a protein. Which is where the last rung leads.

**Information needs difference. A tape with one kind of letter can only count. A tape with four kinds can spell anything, if something can read it.**
