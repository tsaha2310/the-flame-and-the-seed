Ajji writes under Live seed: "copies its tape with about one slip per billion letters; enough to differ, few enough to work." Under Boiled seed: "tape intact, never copied." Under Flame: "nothing to copy."

"Now Kabir's question from Book VII has an answer," she says. "Copying, shown, slips included."

"But the moong does not make cuttings," says Kabir. "It makes seeds. From flowers. With two parents. Why bother, if a cutting is faster?"

"A monk in a garden asked almost that," says Ajji, "and counted twenty-eight thousand pea plants to find out. Tomorrow, the peas."
