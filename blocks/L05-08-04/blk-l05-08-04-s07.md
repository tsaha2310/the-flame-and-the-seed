Ajji writes under Live seed: "burns with air when it can, ferments when it cannot; switches." Under Flame: "needs air; cannot ferment; goes out." Under Boiled seed: "neither."

"Every coin in this Book," says Kabir, "came from glucose falling. The seedling ate its seed halves. They are gone. It is still growing. Where is it getting glucose now?"

Ajji points at the leaves.

"Out of the air," she says. "With light. It is the only uphill reaction in the world that runs on nothing you would call food, and every other fall in this Book depends on it."
