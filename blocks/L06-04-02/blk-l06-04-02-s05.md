Kabir's one-slot ribosome makes single beads. He adds slots until the chain grows.

**Predict first:** can a chain be built?

<!-- STEP id="run" -->
**Turn the dial.** With one slot, a tRNA arrives and pairs, but there is nowhere for the chain to be handed from. Each bead is released alone. No chain ever forms. Two slots can join two beads and then must drop one; three lets the chain be handed forward and the empty tRNA leave in one motion.

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** The three-slot design is the minimum that lets a chain grow without letting go: one to receive, one to hold the chain, one to exit.
