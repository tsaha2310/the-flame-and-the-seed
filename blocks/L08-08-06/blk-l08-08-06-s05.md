Eight mangoes. Kabir has stopped counting and so has everyone else.

"You have every line," says Ajji. "You have every clue. Kabir has knocked down every single one, and he is right about every single one. The seed is the same atoms, the same grips, the same reaction, the same molecules as the boiled seed. It has a wall, a slow fire, a tape, senses, and a copy. And so, in part, does a thermostat, a book, a steam engine, a virus, a mule."

"So there is no answer," says Kabir.

"There is an answer. It is not a line. Tomorrow the rains are over and the moong has pods, and the diya has been out for weeks, and we sit down with the torn page."

She takes the last seed from the jar, the newest, and puts it in Kabir's palm, as she put the first one in yours.
