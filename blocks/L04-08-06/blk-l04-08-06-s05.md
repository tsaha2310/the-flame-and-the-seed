Four mangoes. But Ajji is looking at Kabir differently.

"You said untangle it," she says. "Say why that will not work."

Kabir thinks. "Because nothing is doing the untangling. In the live seed, something is always folding new proteins, and it uses the tape to know the order of the beads, and it needs the coins. The boiled seed has the tape and no coins and no folder."

"So what does the live seed have that the boiled one does not?"

"A wall around it," says Kabir, surprising himself, "so the coins do not float away. And a way to make the coins. And a way to read the tape."

Ajji opens a fresh page. "Book V," she says. "The Slow Fire. The moong is waking. We go inside."
