Distilled water: nothing. Tap water: the faintest glow. Salt water: bright, and brighter the more salt you add. Sugar water: nothing, however much sugar. Nimbu pani: a glow. Mustard oil: nothing.

The flow of charge through the wire and the LED is called a current. You met charge in I.2. A current is charge on the move.

A liquid that carries a current is called an electrolyte. Salt water is a strong one. Nimbu pani is a weak one. Tap water is a very weak one, because of the small amount of dissolved minerals in it. Pure water is not one at all.

Sugar water is the surprise. Sugar dissolves completely, the water goes clear, and still nothing flows. Sugar water and salt water look identical and behave completely differently.

**A current needs charge that can move. Salt water has it. Sugar water does not, however much sugar you add.**
