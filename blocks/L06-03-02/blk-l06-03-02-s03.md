UUU gives a chain of phenylalanine, an oily bead. AAA gives lysine, a plus bead. CCC gives proline. GGG gives glycine. Four codons read.

A repeating pair, UCUCUC, read three at a time, is UCU CUC UCU CUC: two codons alternating. The chain alternates serine and leucine, so UCU and CUC are those two, and one more experiment says which is which.

A repeating triplet, UUCUUC, can be read from three starting points: UUC UUC, UCU UCU, or CUU CUU. Three chains of a single bead each, so three codons assigned at once.

Khorana's chemistry, building any repeating pattern to order, plus Nirenberg's test tube, plus a trick of Nirenberg's for testing single triplets, read all sixty-four by 1966. The full table, which codon names which amino acid, is the genetic code. Khorana and Nirenberg shared the Nobel Prize in 1968.

**The genetic code was read by feeding made-to-order mRNA to a cell's machinery in a test tube and reading what chain came out. All sixty-four codons were assigned within five years.**
