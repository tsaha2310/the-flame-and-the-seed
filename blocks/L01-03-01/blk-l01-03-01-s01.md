Ajji has six saucers, each with a pinch of white powder. They look identical.

"Kitchen salt. Salt-free salt from the chemist, the one with potassium in it. A pinch from the eno sachet. Chuna from the paan shop. Copper sulphate from the garden bag, the blue one, ground fine. And boric powder from the ant box."

She has hidden the labels.

"Sprinkle each one into the flame. Tell me which is which."
