A cell is a bubble with an inside that is different from the outside. Different pH, different concentrations, different molecules, folded and working. Keeping the inside different is what the wall does, and what the coins pay for.

The bubble must be small, because the inside is fed through the skin, and skin does not keep up past a tenth of a millimetre. A mango-sized cell is a thin living shell around a dead core.

The bubble must be a bilayer, because only a two-tailed molecule makes a sheet that closes around water. Soap makes balls with no room inside.

So the moong grows by making more cells, not bigger ones. A seedling is millions of small bubbles, each fed through its own skin, each keeping its own inside different from the outside.

**A living thing is a wall with a different inside. The wall must be small enough to feed through, and a bilayer to have an inside at all.**
