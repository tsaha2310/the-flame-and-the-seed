Kabir's tree of wings puts a bat next to a moth. The tape puts it next to a mouse.

**Predict first:** where do the whale, the bat and the octopus land?

<!-- STEP id="run" -->
**Turn the dial.** The whale lands with fish and sharks; the bat with birds and moths; the octopus, with its camera eye, beside vertebrates. Every one is a false grouping: a feature shaped by the same problem, swimming or flying or seeing, in unrelated lines. The DNA tree scatters them back to their real branches.

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** Similar problems make similar shapes in unrelated lines, from selection. A tree built on those features is a tree of ways of life, not of descent. Inherited features, and above all the tape, tell them apart.
