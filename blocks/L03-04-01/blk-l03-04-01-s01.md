Two steel cups on two burners, same flame under each. One has a cup of water, one a cup of mustard oil. Ajji has a thermometer in each.

Kabir predicts the water will be hotter, "because water boils and oil does not."

After two minutes, the oil is at 80 degrees. The water is at 45.

"Same flame," says Ajji. "Same time. Same amount. Why is one hotter?"
