> **The Lens says:** The wire is cut open and magnified a hundred million times. Electrons are drawn as blue dots and slowed by a trillion. In the water, moving atoms are drawn a thousand times too big. The battery is a drawing.

Your job: keep the LED lit.

Start with the battery push at zero. Nothing moves. Raise it. Watch what moves in the wire.

Now look at the salt-water section. Something else is moving, in two directions.

Take the salt out. The LED dies. Put sugar in instead. Watch what the sugar does, and what it does not do.

Manage the push and the salt so the LED stays lit as brightly as you can without the wire glowing red.
