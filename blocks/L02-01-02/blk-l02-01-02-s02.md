> **The Lens says:** Ions are drawn as balls of two sizes and two colours. Real ions are not balls and have no colour. The board is a slice through the crystal; a real crystal has no edges the atoms can feel until the very surface.

Drop a sodium ion. Drop a chloride ion next to it. Drop another sodium. Try putting it next to the first sodium.

Keep going. The board tells you when a placement is thrown out. Work out the rule.

When you have a square of about 25, rotate the board. Build the next layer up. Then rotate the whole thing and look at the shape you have made.
