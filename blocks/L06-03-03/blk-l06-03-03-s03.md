Every message begins with AUG, which names methionine. AUG is the start codon: it says "begin here, and the first bead is methionine". The machine tomorrow finds it, and reads in threes from there, so AUG also sets which three letters count as a word.

Three codons, UAA, UAG and UGA, name no bead. When the reader reaches one, it stops and lets the chain go. They are stop codons: full stops.

The other sixty-one name twenty beads, so most beads have several codons. Lysine is AAA or AAG. Leucine has six. Usually the codons for one bead differ in the third letter. Naming one bead with several codons is redundancy. Change a codon's third letter, as you just did, and often the bead does not change at all: the message reads the same.

And nearly every living thing uses this same table. A moong seed, a bacterium, a bee, Kabir. The codon AUG means methionine in all of them, which is why a human gene put into a bacterium makes a human protein. The shared table is a clue for Book VIII.
