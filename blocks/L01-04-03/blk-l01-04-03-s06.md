Splitting water is uphill. Burning hydrogen back into water is downhill; it goes with a pop.

So the battery is lifting something. The hydrogen and oxygen you collected are sitting higher on the Hill than the water was.

"Then a gas cylinder," says Kabir slowly, "is a marble at the top of the stairs."

"Write that down," says Ajji. It is the best thing anyone has said all week.

One thread is still loose: the sugar water. Tomorrow you try to break it.
