A new seed in a fresh bowl. Ajji says Book V follows this one from the inside.

"Kabir said the live seed has a wall around it, so the coins do not float away. What is the wall made of?"

"Cellulose," says Kabir. "The beta chain."

"That is the plant's outer wall, and it is a fence, not a skin; water and coins walk straight through it. The skin is under it, and it is made of fat with a phosphate on its head. Build it and see what it does in water."
