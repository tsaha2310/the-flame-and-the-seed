Every rule you tried was a version of the list your school textbook gives. Living things:

- eat
- breathe
- grow
- move
- respond
- reproduce
- die

Put the flame through the list. It passes every line. It takes in oil, it uses air, it grows, it moves, it flinches from wind, it lights other flames, and it goes out.

Now put the moong seed through the list. Dry, in the dabba, it does none of these. Not one. Yet you know it is alive, because next week it will be a plant.

**A checklist of behaviours cannot tell a flame from a seed.**

The seven signs are not wrong. They are things most living things do. They are just not what living things are.
