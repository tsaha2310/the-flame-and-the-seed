Ajji writes "the Hill" at the top of a fresh page and draws a ball on a bumpy line.

"Everything in the seed and everything in the flame is a marble on some hill," she says. "When you cannot see why something happens, ask what fell."

"What fell when the diya burned?" you ask.

She taps the lens. "Something you have not seen yet. It is a kind of falling that does not need a slope."

Tomorrow, a balloon and Kabir's hair.
