> **The Lens says:** A population of ten thousand with the sickle allele at a set starting frequency; malaria as a parasite that kills a share of children each generation, less if they carry the sickle allele, because sickled cells are poor homes for it. Rates are from real studies. Generations pass in seconds.

Run fifty generations with no malaria. Watch the allele frequency. Watch the disease deaths.

Run fifty with malaria. Watch the frequency. Watch both kinds of death. Find where the frequency settles.

Set the frequency to zero with malaria present. Watch total child deaths. Set it very high. Watch.

Find the frequency that gives the fewest total child deaths.
