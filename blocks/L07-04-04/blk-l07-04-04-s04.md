Every signal in this Book was paired with an eraser. Cyclic AMP has an enzyme that cuts it within seconds. mRNA cuts its own backbone within an hour. A neurotransmitter is pulled back or broken down in a millisecond. A pheromone evaporates in minutes. The plant's plume disperses in the wind. Without the eraser, each one becomes a tap that cannot shut, from VI.2 L04: a nerve that fires forever, a liver that pours sugar forever, an ant that walks forever.

The rate of erasing is tuned to the message. A nerve's must be gone in a millisecond so the next spike is clean. An ant trail must last minutes so a trip can reinforce it, and fade in an hour so a moved food source can be abandoned. The eraser's speed is as much part of the signal as the molecule.

**A signal without an eraser is a message that cannot be updated. Every signal in the living world decays, and the decay rate is tuned to how quickly the world it describes changes.**
