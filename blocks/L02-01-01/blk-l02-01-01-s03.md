> **The Lens says:** The atoms are drawn a hundred million times bigger, as balls, which they are not. Charged atoms are coloured; real ones have no colour. The hammer strike is slowed a thousand times.

Strike the copper. Step through the strike one frame at a time. Watch the rows.

Strike the salt. Step through. Watch what sits next to what after the rows shift by one.

Turn the force down and find the smallest strike that splits the salt. Do the same for copper. Does copper ever split?
