A step of "ten times" is a power of ten. Two steps is a hundred times. Three is a thousand. Six steps takes you from a metre to a millionth of a metre, which is where the smallest living things are.

The ladder from Ajji's verandah down to the smallest piece of a seed is about ten rungs. Every Book of this investigation climbs down one or two rungs and then climbs back up.

**Anything that fits on the ladder can be reached. The trick is knowing which rung you are on.**

Here is the whole route, so you can find yourself later.

- Books I to IV go down: the grain, the grip, the blaze, the ladder. Each is a rung or two smaller than the last.
- Books V to VIII come back up: the slow fire, the tape, the whisper, the long game. Each is bigger than the last, all the way to the planet.
