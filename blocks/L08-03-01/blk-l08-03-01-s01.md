"A moong cell has twenty-two chromosomes," says Ajji. "Eleven pairs, one of each pair from the father plant's pollen and one from the mother plant's egg. A seed is a pollen cell joined to an egg cell. So how many chromosomes must a pollen cell carry?"

"Twenty-two," says Kabir. "Cells have twenty-two."

"Then the seed has forty-four. And its children eighty-eight. Run it."
