> **The Lens says:** The cell surface at a hundred million times, in cross-section. Dissolved things are balls of different shapes. Some patches of wall carry shaped proteins drawn as pockets. Bubbles form in real time, slowed a hundred times.

Watch the surface. Some bubbles form anywhere. Tag one and read what is inside.

Some bubbles form only at pits where the wall dimples in. Tag one. Read what is inside. Compare to the bath.

Zoom on a pit before it closes. Look at the proteins on its surface. Look at what is stuck to them.

Change the bath: remove the thing the pits collect. Watch whether pits still form bubbles.
