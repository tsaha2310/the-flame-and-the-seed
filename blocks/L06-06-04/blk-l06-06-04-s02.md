> **The Lens says:** A genome map with a few dozen genes, grouped by what they build; real genomes have thousands of switches per cell type. Switch proteins are drawn as shapes that grip short DNA sequences. Chromatin can be loosened or tightened by region.

Loosen the chromatin around the muscle genes. Place switch proteins on their control sequences. Run. Read which proteins appear.

Tighten the chromatin around the nerve genes. Run. Check they are silent.

Leave the housekeeping genes as they are. Check they run.

Now reset, and from the same tape make a nerve cell. Then a gut cell, from the same tape, with the fewest changes.
