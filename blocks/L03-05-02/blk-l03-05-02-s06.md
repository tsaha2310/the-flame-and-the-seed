Kabir's warm-fizz world has flat fridge drinks. He decides he prefers the real one.

**Predict first:** what happens to a fizzy drink in the fridge?

<!-- STEP id="run" -->
**Turn the dial.** Cold drinks go flat, warm ones fizz. Fish would crowd into hot pools and gasp in cold rivers. A pan of water on the stove would fizz with air as it warmed instead of releasing it.

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** The loose grip water has on a gas molecule is why warmth drives gas out. It is the reason the sea near the poles holds the most life, and why a warm pond is a poor one.
