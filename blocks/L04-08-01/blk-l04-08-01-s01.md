"In 1952," says Ajji, "a scientist in London named Rosalind Franklin pointed X-rays at a fibre of a molecule pulled from calf glands. The rays scattered, and she caught the pattern on film. It was an X, with bars. She labelled it Photo 51."

"What was the molecule?"

"The tape that lasts. Nobody had seen it. She had seen its shadow, and the shadow was enough."
