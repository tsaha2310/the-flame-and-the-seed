Ajji has blown up four long balloons and tied all four at the neck with one knot.

"Hold the knot," she says to Kabir. "Now try to make all four balloons lie flat, like a cross on the table."

He tries. Two lie flat. The third pushes up. When he pushes it down, the fourth pops up. Whatever he does, the four balloons end up pointing at the four corners of a shape he cannot name.

"They do not want to be flat," he says.

"Balloons want nothing," says Ajji. "Try three."
