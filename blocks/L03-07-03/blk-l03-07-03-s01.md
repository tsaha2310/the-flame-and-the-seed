Kabir has heartburn from too much pickle. Ajji stirs a little baking soda into water and he drinks it, making a face. Within a minute he is fine.

"Acid met base," she says. "Hydrogen ions met hydroxide ions. What did they make?"

Kabir thinks. A hydrogen ion is a bare proton. Hydroxide is water missing one. Put them together.

"Water," he says.

"And what is left in the glass?"
