Kabir sets stickiness to zero and cools the box to nothing. The particles hang in space like dust.

"That is the straight-water world," he says.

**Predict first:** with no stickiness at all, what happens when you cool the box to zero?

<!-- STEP id="run" -->
**Turn the dial.** The particles slow down and stop, but never clump. No liquid ever forms; no solid ever forms. The box is a gas at every temperature, and at zero it is a gas of particles standing still.

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** A liquid exists only because of the pulls between molecules. That is why straight water, with almost no pulls, would boil at minus 80.
