Kabir's mango cell rots from the centre. His soap cell is a grease ball with nothing in it.

"Two ways to have no inside," he says.

"And a living thing is exactly a thing with an inside," says Ajji.

**Predict first:** how far in from the skin does oxygen reach in a mango-sized cell?

<!-- STEP id="run" -->
**Turn the dial.** About a tenth of a millimetre. The rest, nearly the whole mango, is dark on the supply map. The centre runs out of oxygen in a minute and out of sugar in an hour; its ATP is not recharged; the acid bags spill; the centre dies and the death spreads outward. Then the soap wall: the molecules make solid balls with no inside. There is no cell to feed. The sim reports: "no interior".

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** A cell must be small enough to feed through its skin, and its wall must be a two-tailed bilayer to have an inside at all. Both are set by the pulls in water.
