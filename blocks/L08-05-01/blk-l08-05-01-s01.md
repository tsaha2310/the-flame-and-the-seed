"Charles Darwin kept pigeons," says Ajji. "So did half of England. Breeders had made fantails and pouters and tumblers from the ordinary rock dove, by choosing which birds to breed from, in a few hundred years. Darwin asked what would choose, in the wild, with no breeder. Alfred Russel Wallace, alone with fever in a hut in the Malay islands, asked the same and wrote the answer in a letter. Be the chooser."
