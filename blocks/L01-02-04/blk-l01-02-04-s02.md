Put one positive charge on the board. The landscape is flat.

Put a negative charge near it. Watch the landscape between them dip.

Drag the negative charge far away. Watch the dip fill in, and the negative charge sit higher on the Hill the further it is dragged.

Now release it. Where does it roll?

Build a landscape with several charges and predict where a released charge ends up.
