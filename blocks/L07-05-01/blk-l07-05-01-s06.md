Kabir's all-alike colony is a green ball that dies in the sun. He lets the switches differ and it grows a root and a leaf.

**Predict first:** how big can the colony get, and what kills it?

<!-- STEP id="run" -->
**Turn the dial.** The colony grows into a ball of identical cells. Every cell photosynthesises, every cell grips at soil, every cell keeps a coat. The inner cells starve in the dark of the ball; the surface cells do poorly at everything; the ball never grows past a millimetre and dies at the first drought.

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** Specialisation is what lets a body be large. A cell that does one job can do it far better than a cell doing all of them, and the signals let the jobs add up.
