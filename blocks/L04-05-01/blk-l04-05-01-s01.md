Ajji heats milk to boiling and squeezes in half a nimbu. The milk splits into white curds and clear whey. She ties the curds in a cloth: paneer.

"The white in milk was dissolved a minute ago," she says. "Now it is a solid you can slice. Nothing was added but a little acid. What is the white made of?"

"Not sugar," says Kabir. "Sugar dissolves and stays dissolved."

"Not sugar. The next rung. Two new elements, and a molecule with a personality."
