You have your first tools: a balance that never lies, ninety elements, and grains too small for the lens.

Ajji wants one more thing before bed. "Every compound has its recipe. Now I want to know why some grains join and some do not. Why does the iron take oxygen, and the gold ring on my finger never does?"

"Because gold is expensive," says Kabir.

That is not it. But to find out, you need to know what pushes and pulls, and what "downhill" means for a grain of iron. That is a marble run, and it is on the stairs tomorrow.
