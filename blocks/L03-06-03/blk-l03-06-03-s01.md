Ajji pours a little hydrogen peroxide from the medicine box into a glass. Nothing. A few slow bubbles.

She drops in a pinch of grated potato. The glass foams over like a science-fair volcano.

She fishes out the potato and drops it into a fresh glass of peroxide. It foams again. The potato is unchanged.

"The potato is not used up," she says. "It is not a reactant. What is it doing?"
