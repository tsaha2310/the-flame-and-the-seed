You have a fingerprint but no finger. Something in each atom produces one exact colour.

Ajji picks up a grain of salt on the tip of a wet finger and holds it under the lens. Cubes. Tiny perfect cubes, all the way down to where the lens goes dark.

"To find what makes the yellow," she says, "you have to see inside one of those. Nobody can. So in 1909 a man in Manchester found another way."

Tomorrow you fire bullets at a box you cannot open.
