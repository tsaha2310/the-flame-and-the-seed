You can read a skeleton. Corners are carbon; hydrogens fill in.

Ajji draws a short zigzag with the letters OH at one end.

"That letter," she says, "is the next rung. But before oxygen, we stay with carbon and hydrogen alone. The gas in the cylinder is nothing else, and neither is the candle, and neither is petrol."

Kabir looks at the cylinder under the counter with sudden interest.
