The bench passes only when a loop closes.

The tape holds the recipes. The ribosome and its translators, built from those recipes, build every machine: the pumps, the enzymes, the staircase, the turbine, the copyist, and the ribosome itself. The staircase and turbine catch a fall, from sugar or from light, as coins. The coins pay the pumps that keep the inside different from the outside, from V.2; the enzymes that build; and the copyist that copies the tape. The copied tape, in a second bubble, builds the machines again.

Take away any one part and the loop opens. No tape: the machines wear out and are not replaced, and the bubble slides to the floor in a day. No turbine: no coins; the pumps stop; the inside levels in an hour. No wall: the coins and the machines drift apart and nothing is anywhere long enough to work. No copyist: one bubble lives its span and there is no second.

**A living thing is a loop. The tape builds the machines. The machines catch energy. The energy keeps the wall and copies the tape. No part is the answer; the loop is.**
