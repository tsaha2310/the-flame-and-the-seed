Ajji has put a glass of water, a spoon of salt, and a globe from Kabir's room on the table.

"One dial," she says. "The angle between water's two hydrogens. 104 degrees in every water molecule that has ever existed. You are going to turn it to 180 and see what happens to all three of these."

"Why 180?" says Kabir.

"Because that is what you got when you switched off the lone pairs. A world where oxygen's spare pairs do not push."
