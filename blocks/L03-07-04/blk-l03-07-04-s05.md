Kabir's all-acid world dissolves seashells. He asks whether the real sea is getting more acid. Ajji says yes, a little, and points at the diya.

**Predict first:** what happens to soil and to the sea?

<!-- STEP id="run" -->
**Turn the dial.** With no basic oxides, nothing cancels the carbon dioxide that every fire and every breath adds. Soil sours and stays sour. The sea drifts to pH 5. Shells dissolve. Chuna becomes an acid, and Ajji's paan is sour.

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** The metal-oxide bases in rock and soil are what has kept the sea near pH 8 for a very long time. Book VIII picks this up.
