> **The Lens says:** Soap drawn as a charged head and a tail, at a hundred million times. Grease is a blob of triglycerides. Water is the Crowd. The micelle in cross-section is real; its perfect roundness is not.

Add real soap to plain water. Watch the cut-away. Watch where the heads go and where the tails go.

Add grease. Stir. Watch the tails. Watch the grease.

Now swap the soap for the two-oily-ends kind. Add to water. Add grease. Stir.

Swap for the two-watery-ends kind. Add. Stir.
