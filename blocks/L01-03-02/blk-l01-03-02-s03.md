> **The Lens says:** The particles are slowed a billion times. The box is drawn cut open so you can see the target; Rutherford never could. The models inside are drawings, not photographs.

Fire a thousand particles at the plum-pudding model. Read the detector ring.

Switch to the nuclear model, a tiny hard positive dot at the centre with almost nothing around it. Fire a thousand again. Read the ring.

Now do what Rutherford's students did: count how many come straight back in each case.
