Heat is jiggle. The fold is held by weak pulls: oily beads squeezed together, charges paired, hydrogen bonds along the backbone. Enough jiggle shakes those apart, and the chain unfolds. Its oily beads, now exposed, are squeezed against the oily beads of every neighbouring unfolded chain, and the chains tangle into a mat. That mat is the egg white, the paneer, the boiled seed. Cool it, and the tangle is stuck; the chain cannot find its way back to its one fold through all its neighbours.

Acid does it another way. Loose hydrogen ions attach to the minus beads and cancel them, and the plus beads gain no partners. Pairs that held the fold let go. The chain loosens and tangles, at room temperature. That is the nimbu in the milk.

A protein that has lost its fold is denatured. Its sequence is intact. Every peptide bond is intact. Every atom is present. It has lost only its shape, and with its shape, its job. The pocket no longer fits.

A protein does its job with its shape: a pocket that fits one molecule and not another, a surface that grips one partner. Shaped pieces that fit or do not fit, and a fit that can bend the piece. That is the fifth model, and its name is Lock and Shape. It joins the Hill, the Tug-of-War and the Crowd on the wall.

**A protein's shape is its job. Heat or acid unfolds the shape, and an unfolded protein has the same atoms and does nothing. That is denaturing, and that is the boiled seed.**
