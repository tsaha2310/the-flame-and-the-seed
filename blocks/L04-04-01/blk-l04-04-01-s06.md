Kabir strips the hydroxyls and his roti turns to candle wax. He puts them back.

**Predict first:** what does the stripped molecule do in water, and what does it taste like?

<!-- STEP id="run" -->
**Turn the dial.** Six carbons with hydrogens only is hexane, a hydrocarbon. It floats on the water like petrol, has no sweetness, and the tongue's detector ignores it. The sim's roti becomes a piece of wax.

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** The hydroxyls are what make glucose a sugar: soluble, sweet, and able to be joined into chains by losing water.
