The sealed jar weighed the same before and after. Nothing got in or out.

When the jar was opened, air rushed in. Something inside had been used up.

The tin, weighed alone, was heavier by exactly the amount of air that rushed in.

The phlogiston story said "lighter". The balance said "heavier". One clean number, and two hundred years of a good story were over.

The part of the air that joins the metal is oxygen. About a fifth of the air is oxygen. Burning is a thing joining oxygen.

And the rule the sealed jar shows has a name: conservation of mass. Whatever happens inside a sealed container, the total mass does not change.

**In a sealed jar, nothing is gained and nothing is lost. Only rearranged.**
