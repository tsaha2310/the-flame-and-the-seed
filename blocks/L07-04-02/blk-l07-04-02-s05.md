**Turn the dial.** A still releases the plume, the wasp still comes, and B does nothing. When the caterpillar's siblings arrive at B, its defences are down, and it loses far more leaf than an alert neighbour. In the sim's field, deaf plants are eaten first.

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** A signal needs a receiver. The plume is only a warning to plants whose receptors fit it, which is why only related plants respond to each other's alarms.
