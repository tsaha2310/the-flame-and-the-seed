"Jean-Baptiste Lamarck, 1809," says Ajji. "A serious scientist, the first to argue that species change at all. His mechanism: a giraffe stretches for high leaves, its neck lengthens a little in its life, and its calves inherit the longer neck. Use it and it grows; disuse it and it shrinks; pass it on. August Weismann tested it in 1889 by cutting the tails off mice and breeding them, for twenty-two generations."

"That is horrible."

"It was a fair test of a fair idea. Run all three worlds."
