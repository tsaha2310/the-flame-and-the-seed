The steel wool got heavier. From 5.0 g to about 5.4 g. Kabir made Ajji do it three times with real wool, and every time the number went up.

The candle and the paper got lighter in open air. But inside the sealed jar, they did not change at all. Everything that left the paper stayed in the jar, as smoke and as gases you cannot see.

So burning does not make stuff disappear. Something from the air joined the wool. Something from the candle escaped into the air. The balance is honest about both.

The amount of stuff a thing is made of has a name: mass. The balance measures it. A gram is a unit of mass.

**Burning moves mass around. It never destroys it and never makes it.**
