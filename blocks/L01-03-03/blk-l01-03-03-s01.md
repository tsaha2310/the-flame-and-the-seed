Ajji puts two glasses of water on the table. They look identical. "One of these is heavier," she says.

Kabir weighs them. She is right. One is a little heavier, and the difference is not the glass.

"It is the same water," says Ajji. "Hydrogen and oxygen, 1 to 8. Same recipe."

"Then how is it heavier?"

"Some of its hydrogen atoms are heavier. Same element. Different weight. Build one and see how."
