The Crowd has now explained melting, boiling, pressure, and a clay pot that makes cold out of nothing but dry air.

One more, and it is the strangest. Ajji drops an ice cube into a glass of water. It floats.

"Every other solid sinks in its own liquid," she says. "Solid ghee sinks in melted ghee. Solid wax sinks in melted wax. Ice floats. And if it did not, there would be no fish."
