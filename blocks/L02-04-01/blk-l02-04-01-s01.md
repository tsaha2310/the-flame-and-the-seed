Ajji drops a spoon of ghee on a roti straight off the tawa. Solid, white, then a clear golden puddle in three seconds.

She holds the roti up. The ghee runs. She waves it in the air and it sets again into a white smear.

"Same ghee," she says. "Solid, liquid, solid. What did I change?"

"You warmed it," says Kabir.

"And what is warm?"
