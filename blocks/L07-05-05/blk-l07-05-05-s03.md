Kabir watches the deaf cell become a lump.

"It is still alive. It is just not part of the body any more."

"That is exactly what it is," says Ajji. "A cheater, from the biofilm, inside a body."

**Predict first:** for each, what fails?

<!-- STEP id="run" -->
**Turn the dial.** The deaf cell divides when its neighbours tell it to stop, because it cannot hear them. Its daughters are deaf too. In a month there is a lump of cells doing no job, pushing the lining aside, taking the blood's sugar, and, if a further change lets them come unstuck, spreading. A body is cells that stick, signal and switch; this cell has stopped listening, and the sim names what it is: a tumour. The plant with no meristems: it keeps the leaves it has, cannot grow, cannot replace a chewed leaf, cannot flower, and dies when its leaves wear out. The one-tip animal: it grows only at its head, cannot mend a wound in its tail, and every organ but the head ages without replacement.

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** A body runs on signals that say stop as much as go. A cell that ignores stop is the beginning of cancer. And a body's reserves must be placed where the body needs mending.
