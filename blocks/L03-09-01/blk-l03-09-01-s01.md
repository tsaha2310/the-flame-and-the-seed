The inverter died at four in the afternoon and the power has not come back. Ajji has taken the cover off the big battery under the stairs. Two heavy lead plates in acid.

"You have seen iron give electrons to copper ions," she says. "The nail went pink. Where did the electrons go?"

"Straight from the iron to the copper. They were touching."

"What if they were not touching? What if the only path was a wire?"

She hands Kabir a copper coin, a galvanised nail, a nimbu and two wires.
