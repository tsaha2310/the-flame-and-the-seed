Kabir has lined up things from the kitchen shelf on the table. Salt. Sugar. An iron nail. A glass of water. A jar of dal-and-rice mixed for khichdi. A balloon of air. The black burned steel wool.

"Which of these are one thing," Ajji asks, "and which are several things pretending to be one?"

Kabir points at the khichdi jar. "That one. Easy. Dal and rice."

"And the water?"

Kabir hesitates. Water is just water. Isn't it?
