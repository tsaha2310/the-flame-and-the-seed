The cookbook: carries information, yes, in exact order. Reads it unaided: no. It waits for a cook. Builds from it: no. Copies it: no; a printer does. Responds: no.

The live seed: carries information, on a tape that lasts. Reads it, with machines made from the tape. Builds from it: every protein, and, through them, every wall, pump and turbine. Copies it: yes, which is Book VIII. Responds: yes, with switches that flip when a signal arrives, which is Book VII.

The boiled seed: carries the same information, letter for letter. Reads it: no; the readers are tangled. Builds: no. Copies: no. Responds: no.

The flame: carries none.
