> **The Lens says:** The cards show only what was known in 1869. Some masses are wrong, as they were then. Six elements are missing from the deck, as they were then. Nothing is drawn smaller than a card.

Lay the cards out in one long line, lightest first.

Now look for repeats. Lithium, sodium, potassium: all soft metals that fizz in water. Fluorine, chlorine, bromine: all sharp-smelling, all greedy for metals.

Start a new row every time the pattern repeats, so that look-alikes sit in a column.

Some columns will have a hole in them. Leave the hole. Do not force a card into it. Then use the column to predict what the missing element should weigh and how it should behave.
