Water has a negative corner and two positive corners. Oil has no corners of any kind.

Now put a million water molecules in a bowl. The negative corner of one, the positive corner of the next. What do they do?

Ajji drops water onto a one-rupee coin, one drop at a time, and Kabir counts. The pile of water on the coin grows into a dome that seems to defy sense. Twenty drops. Thirty. Still holding.

"Now oil," she says. Three drops, and it runs off the edge.
