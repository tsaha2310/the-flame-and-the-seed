> **The Lens says:** The nucleus drawn as a library, the DNA as one chained book; both are cartoons of a bubble and a thread. The copyist is a real enzyme, drawn as a wheel. The copy it makes is drawn as a strip of letters.

Pick a recipe. Watch the copyist find it and open the book there. Watch it write. Read the copy's letters beside the recipe's.

Send the copy out. Watch the master. Is it changed?

Make ten copies of one recipe. Watch them leave. Watch what happens to each after an hour.
