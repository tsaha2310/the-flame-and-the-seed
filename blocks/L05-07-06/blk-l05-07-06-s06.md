Kabir's atoms-into-energy world breaks Lavoisier. He has known since Book I that it would.

**Predict first:** what does the balance check say in this world?

<!-- STEP id="run" -->
**Turn the dial.** Ten kilograms of atoms vanish. The balance from I.1 flags it: mass destroyed. No carbon dioxide is breathed out, and the man's blood, with no carbon dioxide, has no buffer. He is ten kilograms lighter and cannot regulate his blood's pH.

<!-- STEP id="nails" -->
**What this nails:** Mass is conserved through every step of metabolism. The Hill accounts for the energy; the balance accounts for the atoms. Both must close.
