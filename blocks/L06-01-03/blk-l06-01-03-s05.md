Kabir's unspooled nucleus is a ball of tangled thread. He spools it and the map appears.

**Predict first:** does the thread fit, and can any part be read?

**Turn the dial.** Two metres of loose thread in a five-micrometre room. It tangles at once. Nothing can find the start of any gene, and copying it, previewed, ties knots. The sim's cell cannot read its own tape.
